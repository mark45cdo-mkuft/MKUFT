## Scope of the paper

The paper advances a constraint-first procedural/systems interpretation of the Voynich Manuscript. It treats the script as a candidate operator/state-transition system rather than assuming natural-language semantics, and proposes tests based on line bounds, constrained glyph deformation, repeated modules, and image/process alignment.

The record of publication is not itself proof that the interpretation is correct. The scientific claims remain subject to independent testing and competing explanations.
