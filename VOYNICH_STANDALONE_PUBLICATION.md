## What v2.0 consolidates

The v2.0 paper consolidates the recovered Voynich / ESRT / ESF research stack into one publication-ready object. Its governing scientific claim is narrower than a translation or solved-manuscript claim: the Voynich Manuscript should be tested as a candidate multimodal procedural/executable information system in which **Addressing, State and Flow** may carry load-bearing structure alongside — or potentially underneath — language-like token statistics.

The consolidation incorporates the recovered engine proof stack, standalone paper lineage, ESRT formal method, structural-engine method, map key, practice/EMC material, terminology control, and current public MKUFT modules 07, 09 and 10. Earlier exploratory claims remain visible as historical results where appropriate, but current claim boundaries, ordinary alternatives, held-out tests, perturbation controls and falsifiers govern promotion.

## Claim boundary

The current paper does **not** claim:

- a translation of the Voynich Manuscript;
- proven original historical use;
- that glyphs have been demonstrated to be executable operators;
- a demonstrated metaphysical mechanism;
- that linguistic, cipher, scribal, mnemonic, diagrammatic or decorative alternatives have been eliminated;
- or that the Voynich application validates MKUFT physical claims.

The decisive burden is prospective and comparative: preregistered geometry, blinded coding, raw tables, rotation/shift/shuffle/ablation controls, matched manuscripts and held-out folios must show that the proposed Address / State / Flow relations carry predictive load beyond the strongest ordinary alternatives.
