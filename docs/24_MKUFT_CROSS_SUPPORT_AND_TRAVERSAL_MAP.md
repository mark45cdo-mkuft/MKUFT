## Purpose

MKUFT contains several kinds of public document: foundational proposals, mathematical scaffolds, experimental programmes, falsifiers, explanatory bridges, and applied research branches. This map shows how those public objects support and constrain one another.

Its central rule is:

> A module should be read with the definitions, evidence requirements, ordinary alternatives, and falsifiers that limit its claims.

The map is navigation and dependency structure. It does not create evidence, establish a physical mechanism, or replace the scientific content of the linked modules.

## Structural nodes and canonical handles

### 21 — Ambiguity Dynamics

[Ambiguity Dynamics and Manoeuvre Space](21_AMBIGUITY_DYNAMICS_AND_MANOEUVRE_SPACE.md) separates unresolved state volume, route connectivity, and preserved access:

$$
A_{t,\mathrm{vol}}^{(d)}
=
\log\!\left(1+\frac{\mu_d(\Omega_t^{(d)})}{\mu_{0,d}}\right),
$$

$$
M_t=A_{t,\mathrm{vol}}^{(d)}R_tX_t.
$$

$M_t$ is a heuristic audit index, not a validated universal law.

### 22 — Cross-Layer Invariants

[Cross-Layer Invariants and Layer Addressing](22_CROSS_LAYER_INVARIANTS_AND_LAYER_ADDRESSING.md) represents a candidate invariant at layer $L$ as

$$
K_L=A_L(K;\theta_L),
$$

with typed coupling

$$
C_{LM}:\mathcal X_L\rightarrow\mathcal X_M.
$$

Repeated algebraic form does not establish a shared physical mechanism.

### 24A — Active Traversal

[Active Traversal and Functional Emergence](24A_ACTIVE_TRAVERSAL_AND_FUNCTIONAL_EMERGENCE_HYPOTHESIS.md) tests whether structured active traversal improves a predeclared functional score relative to matched controls:

$$
\Delta F(q)=F[\Psi_{\mathrm{struct}}(q)]-F[\Psi_{\mathrm{control}}(q)].
$$

The hypothesis requires a positive expected difference under matched information, compute, retrieval, prompting, and evaluation conditions.

### 24B — Strongest Fair Null

[Strongest Fair Null and Relational Specificity](24B_STRONGEST_FAIR_NULL_AND_RELATIONAL_SPECIFICITY.md) defines the strongest fair scalar null as

$$
F_{\mathrm{null}}^{*}
=
\max\!\left\{
F_A^{*},
F_B^{*},
F_{\mathrm{ind}}^{*},
F_{A\to B}^{*},
F_{B\to A}^{*},
F_{\mathrm{replay}}^{*}
\right\},
$$

with

$$
G_{\mathrm{syn}}^{*}=F_{AB}-F_{\mathrm{null}}^{*},
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{path}}=F_{AB}-F_{\mathrm{replay}}^{*}.
$$

Relationship specificity is tested against a preregistered set of serious alternatives rather than a deliberately weak comparator.

### 25 — Load-Bearing Deformation

[Load-Bearing Invariants and Whole-System Deformation](25_LOAD_BEARING_INVARIANTS_AND_WHOLE_SYSTEM_DEFORMATION.md) compares a baseline with controlled deformations. One compact scaffold is

$$
\Delta X_r=X_{\mathrm{baseline}}-X_{\mathrm{deformed}}(r),
$$

with a domain-defined deformation vector whose coordinates must retain an explicit sign convention. Structural centrality is not treated as truth.

### 26 — Layer Before Law

[Layer Before Law](26_LAYER_BEFORE_LAW_MKUFT_QUANTUM_GRAVITY_REFRAMING.md) uses the typed cycle

$$
S\rightarrow I\rightarrow P\rightarrow O\rightarrow S
$$

and a placeholder update map

$$
U_n\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Update}_{\mathrm{SIPO}}}U_{n+1}.
$$

The update symbol does not constitute a completed dynamical law. The branch must still satisfy Bell compatibility, no-signalling, standard-physics recovery, and discriminating comparison.

### 27 — Typed Traversal and Equation Hygiene

[Typed Traversal and Equation Hygiene](27_TYPED_TRAVERSAL_AND_EQUATION_HYGIENE.md) keeps component spaces typed:

$$
S_n\in\mathcal X_S,
\qquad
I_n\in\mathcal X_I,
\qquad
P_n\in\mathcal X_P,
\qquad
O_n\in\mathcal X_O.
$$

It prevents physical geometry, state-space geometry, information adjacency, observer registration, and metaphysical language from being silently flattened into one mathematical object.

## Public boundary

The public repository contains scientific proposals, mathematical and methodological material, public explanations, provenance and rights information, navigation, and deliberately published archives. A public dependency map describes those objects for the reader; it does not expose private operating instructions or private reasoning architecture.

## Compressed rule

> Define the claim at its proper layer, route it to its mathematical and evidential support, keep its falsifier attached, and do not let structural importance, mathematical elegance, or local performance silently become empirical truth.
