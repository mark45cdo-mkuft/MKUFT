## Purpose

MKUFT contains several kinds of public document: foundational proposals, mathematical scaffolds, experimental programmes, falsifiers, explanatory bridges, and applied research branches. This map shows how those public objects support and constrain one another.

Its central rule is:

> A module should be read with the definitions, evidence requirements, ordinary alternatives, and falsifiers that limit its claims.

The map is navigation and dependency structure. It does not create evidence, establish a physical mechanism, or replace the scientific content of the linked modules.

### 31 — Context-Conditioned State Comparison and Observability

[Context-Conditioned State Comparison and Observability](31_CONTEXT_CONDITIONED_STATE_COMPARISON_AND_OBSERVABILITY.md) constrains counterfactuals and cross-implementation comparisons by requiring a declared comparison class, measurement-relative state equivalence, target-relative consequence spread, and support-compatible dynamics.

$$
D[\mathcal A_X]\in\mathcal M_X.
$$

Measurement-relative equivalence is

$$
x\sim_O x'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal R_{O,q}(x)=\mathcal R_{O,q}(x')
\quad\forall q\in\mathcal Q_O^{\mathrm{adm}}.
$$

For target $Y$, consequence diameter is

$$
\Delta_Y(\Omega_t)
=
\sup_{z,z'\in\Omega_t}
d_Y(F_Y(z),F_Y(z')).
$$

The module does not create a new layer or substrate claim. It prevents a counterfactual from changing the object class while pretending everything else remained fixed, and prevents layer-specific closure from being confused with complete measurement resolution.

$$

with schematic adaptive update

$$
\mathfrak F_t(\mathfrak A_t,u_t)
=
(\alpha_{t+1},x_{t+1})
\in\mathfrak X.
$$

When the construction address changes, before/after state subtraction or equivalence requires a lawful comparison map. A possible scale-separated enabling-constraint signature is

$$

\qquad

where the first observable measures lower-scale feasible-state volume and the second higher-scale viable capability, and each difference is valid only inside a declared common comparison space.

The module does not equate constraint count with coherence or freedom, does not infer dynamics from recurrent morphology, and does not establish independent I→P physics unless an additional information-layer relation survives the adequate P-state/history baseline.
