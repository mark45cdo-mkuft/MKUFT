## 1. Purpose

Module 32 was developed from an independently reached chain of reasoning about recursive learning, self-maintained constraint, changing future reach, and scale-dependent freedom/capability. A later literature audit found substantial prior and adjacent work. The scientific task is therefore not to defend every ingredient as new. It is to identify what prior work already contains, what Module 32 adds operationally, and what remains a candidate original contribution pending broader literature review and empirical use.

The governing rule is:

> Independent convergence can support confidence in a reasoning route, but scientific novelty belongs only to the residual structure not already supplied by prior work.

## 2. Strong prior art that removes broad novelty claims

### 2.1 Closure of constraints is prior work

Montévil and Mossio (2015) formally characterise biological organisation as closure of constraints: constraints act on processes, remain relatively conserved at the relevant time scale, and mutually depend on and maintain one another.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of constraint closure, self-maintaining constraint networks, or the idea that organised constraints can help constitute a biological system.

### 2.2 Changing biological possibility/phase spaces are prior work

Longo, Montévil, and Kauffman (2012) argue that biological evolution changes the relevant phase space itself, including the observables and boundary conditions that can be specified. Kauffman and Roli (2021) develop the same open-ended problem in terms of ever-changing biological phase spaces and newly created possibilities.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of changing possibility spaces, changing observables, the adjacent possible, or the general distinction between moving inside a fixed space and evolution that changes what possibilities exist.

### 2.3 Internally maintained constraints altering accessible-state topology are close prior work

Adams, Jacopin, Gagrani, and Witkowski (2024) explicitly study internal and external constraints, physically accessible state sets, changes in state-space topology, internally maintained biological constraints, and the possibility that such constraints generate new local or emergent rules. Their paper is a particularly close antecedent to the constraint → accessible-state / rule-change portion of Module 32.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** the broad claim that endogenous constraints change accessible states or apparent local rules is not attributed to Module 32 as a first discovery.

### 2.4 Semantic/symbol–matter control is prior work

Pattee (2001) develops the symbol–matter or epistemic-cut problem and treats physical constraints as necessary to measurement, control, and coding across that cut.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of semantic closure, physical symbol control, or the general idea that informational organisation can be physically realised through constraints.

### 2.5 Agency/open-endedness has already been linked to future possibility spaces

López-Díaz and Gershenson (2026) develop a graded organisational theory in which agency involves endogenous anticipatory modulation and open-endedness begins when organisation can reconstruct its own future space of possibilities.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** reconstruction of future possibility spaces is adjacent contemporary work and cannot be presented as unique to Module 32.

## 4. Strongest candidate contribution after the audit

The audit does not support the statement that Module 32 invented constraint closure, enablement, changing phase spaces, endogenous constraints, accessible-state topology, semantic closure, or reconstruction of future possibility spaces.

It does support a narrower claim:

> No single pre-existing source located in this audit supplies the same operational conjunction of (i) an explicit changing construction/state-space address, (ii) a mandatory comparison map before state parity or subtraction across changed addresses, (iii) current organisation as the parameterising object for admissibility, (iv) recursive update of reachable-state geometry, (v) separate lower-scale feasible-state and higher-scale viable-capability observables including an overconstraint control, and (vi) typed recurrence/deformation and strongest-fair-null tests inside one reusable adaptive-system formalism.

That conjunction is therefore recorded as a **candidate original formal synthesis and operationalisation by Mark Charles McLaughlin**, publicly formulated on 15 August 2026, subject to revision if earlier equivalent prior art is located.

This wording is intentionally narrower than “new theory of life”, “new law”, or “new mathematics”. It identifies the contribution at the level the present evidence can support.

## 5. Why the scale-separated assay matters

The originating intuition that restriction of lower-level components can enable greater whole-system capability is not itself new. The contribution candidate is the insistence that the two claims be measured on different declared observables rather than compressed into one untyped notion of freedom.

When lawful before/after comparison exists at each scale, an enabling pattern can be represented as

$$
\Delta\mu_\ell < 0,
\qquad
\Delta\nu_L > 0,
$$

where `mu_l` measures lower-scale feasible-state extent and `nu_L` measures higher-scale viable capability.

The necessary overconstraint control is

$$
\Delta\mu_\ell < 0,
\qquad
\Delta\nu_L < 0.
$$

The scientific point is not that constraints are beneficial. It is that **restriction and capability are different scale-indexed observables**, and a constraint earns an enabling interpretation only if the predicted higher-scale capability actually appears.

## 6. Why the addressed comparison guard matters

A system may change the variables, decoder, rule set, action repertoire, topology, or construction by which its state is defined. Module 32 writes that explicitly using an address `alpha_t` and an addressed family `{X_alpha}`.

The mathematical ingredients are standard. The proposed methodological contribution is to make the consequence unavoidable in adaptive-system reasoning:

> If `alpha_(t+1) != alpha_t`, then expressions such as `x_(t+1) - x_t`, distance, improvement, preservation of state, or “same object” are not automatically licensed. A translation, embedding, quotient, common observable, or other declared comparison map must be supplied.

This is the adaptive counterpart of MKUFT's broader constitutive-state-parity discipline.

The present audit supports the phrases:

- **no equivalent full conjunction located in the primary literature searched on 15 August 2026**;
- **publicly formulated by Mark Charles McLaughlin by the timestamped Module 32 commit**.

It does not yet support:

- first person ever to describe changing possibility spaces;
- first theory of endogenous constraint;
- first mathematical treatment of systems on different state spaces;
- proof that the conjunction is globally unprecedented;
- proof that the formalism is empirically superior;
- proof of consciousness, life, I→P dynamics, or a new physical law.

## 8. What would upgrade or downgrade the novelty claim

The novelty claim should be upgraded only if a broader systematic review across theoretical biology, artificial life, control theory, category/stratified-state methods, meta-learning, dynamical systems, and philosophy of science fails to locate an equivalent conjunction and if the formalism demonstrates practical discriminating value.

It should be downgraded immediately if an earlier source is found that contains the same functional conjunction, even under different terminology.

A partial predecessor does not erase the residual contribution; it changes the boundary of what is attributable.

The novelty audit narrows Module 32 but does not reduce it to rediscovery. Several broad ideas are clearly prior work. The strongest defensible residual is a **specific addressed, scale-separated, deformation-tested formal synthesis for adaptive systems whose organisation can change their later admissibility and reachable-state geometry**.

That residual is significant enough to preserve canonically and investigate empirically, while remaining deliberately open to correction by earlier literature or stronger comparison models.
