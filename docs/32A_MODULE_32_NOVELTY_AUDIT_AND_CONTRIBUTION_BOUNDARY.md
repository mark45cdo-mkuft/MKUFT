## 1. Purpose

Module 32 was developed from an independently reached chain of reasoning about recursive learning, self-maintained constraint, changing future reach, and scale-dependent freedom/capability. Same-day refinement then produced explicit load-bearing-set tests, carrier-substitution tests, relational addressability, and a same-self parity guard for changing individuality addresses.

A literature audit found substantial prior and adjacent work. The scientific task is therefore not to defend every ingredient as new. It is to identify what prior work already contains, what MKUFT adds operationally, and what remains a candidate original contribution pending broader literature review and empirical use.

The governing rule is:

> Independent convergence can support confidence in a reasoning route, but scientific novelty belongs only to the residual structure not already supplied by prior work.

A second governing rule was added during this audit cycle:

> A new canon write is itself a changed object. Re-audit the integrated object; if the write exposes another load-bearing relation, continue refinement rather than treating the successful commit as closure.

This second rule is process provenance, not a novelty claim about scientific method.

## 2. Strong prior art that removes broad novelty claims

### 2.1 Closure of constraints is prior work

Montévil and Mossio (2015) formally characterise biological organisation as closure of constraints: constraints act on processes, remain relatively conserved at the relevant time scale, and mutually depend on and maintain one another.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of constraint closure, self-maintaining constraint networks, or the idea that organised constraints can help constitute a biological system.

### 2.2 Changing biological possibility/phase spaces are prior work

Longo, Montévil, and Kauffman (2012) argue that biological evolution changes the relevant phase space itself, including the observables and boundary conditions that can be specified. Kauffman and Roli (2021) develop the same open-ended problem in terms of ever-changing biological phase spaces and newly created possibilities.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of changing possibility spaces, changing observables, the adjacent possible, or the general distinction between moving inside a fixed space and evolution that changes what possibilities exist.

### 2.3 Internally maintained constraints altering accessible-state topology are close prior work

Adams, Jacopin, Gagrani, and Witkowski (2024) explicitly study internal and external constraints, physically accessible state sets, changes in state-space topology, internally maintained biological constraints, and the possibility that such constraints generate new local or emergent rules. Their paper is a particularly close antecedent to the constraint → accessible-state / rule-change portion of Module 32.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** the broad claim that endogenous constraints change accessible states or apparent local rules is not attributed to Module 32 as a first discovery.

### 2.4 Semantic/symbol–matter control is prior work

Pattee (2001) develops the symbol–matter or epistemic-cut problem and treats physical constraints as necessary to measurement, control, and coding across that cut.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** Module 32 does not claim invention of semantic closure, physical symbol control, or the general idea that informational organisation can be physically realised through constraints.

### 2.5 Agency/open-endedness has already been linked to future possibility spaces

López-Díaz and Gershenson (2026) develop a graded organisational theory in which agency involves endogenous anticipatory modulation and open-endedness begins when organisation can reconstruct its own future space of possibilities.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** reconstruction of future possibility spaces is adjacent contemporary work and cannot be presented as unique to Module 32.

### 2.6 Individuality and organism–environment coupling are strong prior art

Autopoiesis and later autonomy/enaction work already treat individuality as an organised, maintained distinction rather than a mere inventory of parts. Varela, Maturana and Uribe (1974) formalise autopoietic organisation as an autonomous unity. Di Paolo (2005) develops adaptivity as regulation relative to conditions of viability. Moreno and Etxeberria (2005) explicitly treat self-construction and environmental activity as aspects of one organisation in basic autonomous systems. Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde (2009) require an agent to define its own individuality and describe agency as autonomous organisation adaptively regulating its coupling with the environment. Aguilera and Di Paolo (2018; 2019) model delimitation of an integrated unit from its environment and adaptive preservation of integrity under environmental change.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** 32S1 does not claim invention of relational individuality, organism–environment coupling, autonomy, self-maintenance, or the idea that boundaries and environmental relations can matter to identity.

### 2.7 Ablation, necessary-condition tests, multiple realisation, coarse-graining and macro variables are established methods

Removing a candidate component or relation to test causal necessity, comparing alternative physical realisations of a function, coarse-graining lower-level variables, and constructing effective macro variables all have large prior literatures.

**Consequence for MKUFT:** 32S does not claim invention of ablation, minimal sufficient sets, multiple realisation, quotient descriptions, predictive macro variables, or the general idea that a higher-level function may survive a change of carrier.

## 4. Strongest candidate contribution after the expanded audit

The audit does not support the statement that Module 32 or its supplements invented constraint closure, enablement, changing phase spaces, endogenous constraints, accessible-state topology, semantic closure, reconstruction of future possibility spaces, relational individuality, organism–environment coupling, ablation, multiple realisation, quotient spaces, or coarse-graining.

It does support a narrower candidate:

> No single pre-existing source located in this audit supplies the same operational conjunction of (i) an explicit changing construction/state-space address, (ii) a mandatory comparison map before state parity or subtraction across changed addresses, (iii) current organisation as the parameterising object for admissibility, (iv) recursive update of reachable-state geometry, (v) separate lower-scale feasible/addressable-state and higher-scale viable-capability observables including an overconstraint control, (vi) minimal load-bearing/deformation and relation-versus-carrier tests, and (vii) a same-self parity guard requiring lawful identity continuation when constitutive boundary, environment coupling, or the identity-equivalence rule changes.

That conjunction is recorded as a **candidate original formal synthesis and operationalisation by Mark Charles McLaughlin**, with its parts timestamped according to their actual public commits on 15 August 2026 and subject to revision if earlier equivalent prior art is located.

This wording is intentionally narrower than `new theory of life`, `new law`, `new mathematics`, or `first relational theory of self`. It identifies the contribution at the resolution the present evidence can support.

## 5. Why the scale-separated assay matters

The intuition that restriction of lower-level components can enable greater whole-system capability is not itself new. The contribution candidate is the insistence that the two claims be measured on different declared observables rather than compressed into one untyped notion of freedom.

When lawful before/after comparison exists at each scale, an enabling pattern can be represented as

$$
\Delta\mu_\ell < 0,
\qquad
\Delta\nu_L > 0.
$$

The necessary overconstraint control is

$$
\Delta\mu_\ell < 0,
\qquad
\Delta\nu_L < 0.
$$

The scientific point is not that constraints are beneficial. It is that **restriction and capability are different scale-indexed observables**, and a constraint earns an enabling interpretation only if the predicted higher-scale capability actually appears.

## 6. Why the addressed comparison and same-self guards matter

A system may change the variables, decoder, rule set, action repertoire, topology, construction, boundary, or system–environment coupling by which its state and individuality are defined.

The mathematical ingredients are standard. The proposed methodological contribution is to make two consequences explicit inside one adaptive audit:

> If the construction/state-space address changes, subtraction, distance, improvement, preservation of state, or `same object` is not automatically licensed. A translation, embedding, quotient, common observable, or other declared comparison map must be supplied.

and, after 32S1:

> If the identity address changes because a constitutive boundary, environment coupling, maintenance relation, or identity-equivalence rule changes, `the same self lost freedom` is also not automatically licensed. Identity continuation requires its own lawful map or invariant test.

The second rule is not a claim that the environment is always part of the self. It is a guard against silently holding the identity criterion fixed when the relations that constitute that criterion have changed.

## 9. What would upgrade or downgrade the novelty claim

The novelty claim should be upgraded only if a broader systematic review across theoretical biology, artificial life, control theory, dynamical systems, multiscale modelling, causal emergence, autonomy/enaction, category/stratified-state methods, meta-learning, and philosophy of science fails to locate an equivalent conjunction and if the formalism demonstrates practical discriminating value.

It should be downgraded immediately if an earlier source is found that contains the same functional conjunction, even under different terminology.

A partial predecessor does not erase the residual contribution; it changes the boundary of what is attributable.

## 11. Current verdict

The expanded novelty audit narrows the Module 32 family but does not reduce it to rediscovery. Several broad ideas and several mathematical ingredients are clearly prior work.

The strongest defensible residual is a **specific addressed, scale-separated, deformation-tested adaptive formalism in which organisation can change later admissibility and reachable-state geometry, lower-scale restriction is separated from higher-scale capability, load-bearing relations are tested by ablation/substitution, and changes in the very criterion of individuality trigger a same-self comparison guard rather than being silently treated as one fixed self losing freedom.**

That residual is significant enough to preserve canonically and investigate empirically, while remaining deliberately open to correction by earlier literature, stronger comparison models, or failed experiments.
