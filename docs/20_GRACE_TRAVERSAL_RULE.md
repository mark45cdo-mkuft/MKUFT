GRACE is the compact route check:

- **Geometry** — define shape and boundary.
- **Relation** — identify typed connections.
- **Admissibility** — identify permitted transitions.
- **Coherence** — test consistency under declared constraints.
- **Emergence** — identify the checkable outcome.

GRACE adds no new S–I–P–O layer and supplies no evidence by itself.
