**Status:** governing scientific-claim discipline for the physics-facing, mathematical, and empirical MKUFT stack. It reports no confirmed new physical effect.

## 1. Purpose

MKUFT contains a broad architecture, a mathematical scaffold, experimental proposals, systems methods, and speculative branches. Breadth can create a false impression that every component carries the same evidential status.

This module prevents that failure.

Its governing rule is:

> Separate architectural novelty, mathematical validity, empirical support, and physical mechanism. Strength in one category does not silently transfer into another.

Where older public wording conflicts with this module on claim status, equation status, novelty, flagship priority, or speculative-application boundaries, this module governs that narrow axis.

## 2. Four independent questions

Every MKUFT claim must answer four distinct questions.

### 2.1 Is the architecture coherent?

This concerns:

- typed S–I–P–O addressing;
- explicit relations and boundaries;
- reduction rules;
- falsifiers;
- promotion gates;
- controlled deformation;
- internal consistency.

A coherent architecture is valuable, but coherence alone does not establish physical truth.

### 2.2 Is the mathematics valid?

This concerns:

- defined spaces and objects;
- dimensional consistency;
- normalisation;
- positivity where probabilities are used;
- valid operators;
- compatible units;
- declared approximation regimes;
- well-posed dynamics.

Valid notation does not by itself establish that nature uses the model.

### 2.3 Is there empirical support?

This concerns:

- preregistered predictions;
- adequate power and precision;
- strong ordinary nulls;
- blinding and instrumentation;
- held-out prediction;
- independent replication;
- effect-size stability;
- publication of null results.

A broad anomaly does not establish MKUFT unless the framework predicted the distinguishing fingerprint beforehand.

### 2.4 Is there a physical mechanism?

This requires:

- physical state variables;
- units and observables;
- dynamical equations;
- couplings;
- symmetries and conservation conditions;
- recovery of accepted limits;
- at least one discriminating quantitative consequence.

A layer name, tuple, weighting term, projection, or update-map name is not a completed physical mechanism.

## 3. Equation-status labels

Every quantitative expression should be read under one of the following labels.

### E0 — notation or bookkeeping

A symbol or relation used to keep concepts distinct.

Examples:

- S, I, P, O layer addresses;
- typed source and receiving spaces;
- an index naming an experimental condition.

E0 is not a physical law.

### E1 — heuristic scaffold

A mathematically legal candidate form that organises a hypothesis but has not been physically derived or empirically validated.

Examples include, unless later promoted:

```text
W_tilde(E) = integral of physical, information-weighting, and observer-condition terms
```

```text
C_O = C_0 [1 + epsilon kappa h]
```

```text
C[gamma] = integral of local path cost
```

```text
F_boundary = gradient term + rotational term + interaction potential
```

E1 equations may guide operationalisation. They must not be described as established equations of nature.

### E2 — operational statistical model

A fully specified model with measurable variables, units or declared normalisation, frozen estimation rules, uncertainty treatment, and a defined dataset.

E2 can be tested and compared with baselines.

### E3 — replicated empirical model

An E2 model that predicts held-out results and survives independent replication with stable parameter relationships.

E3 supports the tested empirical relation only.

### E4 — derived physical mechanism

A model with defined physical states, dynamics, couplings, symmetries, conservation conditions, accepted-limit recovery, and a discriminating quantitative prediction.

No current MKUFT foundational equation should be described as E4 until those requirements are actually met.

## 4. Novelty discipline

MKUFT should not claim novelty merely because familiar mathematics is written in new notation.

### 4.1 Not individually new

The following ingredients are established mathematical or scientific tools:

- measure spaces and probability spaces;
- L-squared function spaces;
- graph and trajectory representations;
- Gibbs-like or exponential cost weighting;
- linear-response perturbations;
- anisotropic quadratic forms;
- threshold, bifurcation, membrane, and order-parameter language;
- ablation, model comparison, and preregistration.

Using them is legitimate. Their presence does not by itself constitute new physics.

### 4.2 Plausible MKUFT originality

The strongest novelty claim is presently architectural and methodological:

- typed S–I–P–O layer-address discipline;
- explicit prevention of cross-layer evidence transfer;
- promotion gates from assay to discriminating signature;
- strongest-fair-null and full-history replay controls;
- controlled deformation of supposedly load-bearing relations;
- integration of path cost, observer condition, recovery limits, and ontology boundaries inside one research architecture;
- ATLD reciprocal-traversal testing as a functional systems hypothesis.

This may be original as a synthesis, research programme, or systems architecture even before a new physical law is demonstrated.

### 4.3 Required wording

Prefer:

> MKUFT proposes an original layered research architecture using established mathematical tools.

Do not say:

> These standard mathematical forms prove a newly discovered unified field.

## 5. Claim ladder

Public statements should use the narrowest accurate level.

1. **Conceptual proposal** — an organised explanatory architecture.
2. **Mathematical scaffold** — candidate formal relations with explicit limitations.
3. **Operational hypothesis** — variables and measurements are defined.
4. **Discriminating prediction** — a locked fingerprint differs from strong alternatives.
5. **Replicated empirical relation** — independent confirmation exists.
6. **Mechanism-level result** — a parameterised mechanism explains and predicts the tested effect.
7. **Foundational physical theory** — accepted regimes are recovered and new quantitative physics is demonstrated.

Current MKUFT components occupy different levels. The framework as a whole must not be promoted to level 7 by a result that only reaches level 3, 4, or 5 in one branch.

## 6. Experimental priority reset

The empirical programme remains broad, but public emphasis should follow evidential leverage rather than spectacle.

### Priority A — near-term operational flagship

**ATLD reciprocal architecture / strongest fair null / full-history replay**

Why:

- variables and controls can be operationalised now;
- strong alternative explanations can be directly tested;
- reset, replay, one-way, independent-ensemble, and relation-scramble controls are available;
- success would establish a real systems result without requiring speculative physics;
- failure would cleanly constrain the architecture.

### Priority B — foundational derivation programme

**Layer Before Law quantum and gravitational recovery**

Why:

- it addresses the central foundational claim;
- it cannot hide behind anomalous data;
- Bell compatibility, no-signalling, Born-rule recovery, QFT limits, gravitational recovery, and a new discriminator are mandatory;
- failure can reduce the branch to interpretation rather than physical mechanism.

### Priority C — controlled physical residual bridge

**One apparatus, one residual equation, one parameter sweep**

Preferred domains:

- precision noise and boundary conditions;
- dielectric, acoustic, photonic, fluid, plasma, or material-transition systems;
- geometry and threshold effects only after ordinary models are fully specified.

The experiment must predict a residual response surface, not merely report that geometry matters.

### Priority D — reconnaissance hooks

- RNG/REG;
- remote information;
- collective correlations;
- low-frequency physiological exposure.

These may remain useful assays and branch falsifiers. They are not the strongest public proof route and must not carry the theory's credibility.

## 7. RNG and observer-effect boundary

The RNG branch is a simple accessible hook, not a unified-field proof.

A positive generic deviation would establish neither:

- consciousness causing collapse;
- a substrate;
- a unified field;
- MKUFT as a whole.

Promotion requires a preregistered interaction fingerprint involving defined source class, information route, measured state, negative controls, held-out confirmation, and independent replication.

Standard quantum measurement must not be described as proof that conscious watching changes reality. Ordinary measurement interaction and decoherence remain the baseline.

The MKUFT-specific question is whether a measured observer-state variable adds predictive value beyond apparatus, environment, expectation, and accepted physical models.

## 8. Boundary and speculative-application firewall

The physics-facing MKUFT core excludes unsupported aerospace, UAP, anomalous-craft, fixed-angle, and shape-specific motion claims.

General boundary mechanics may examine:

- measured gradients;
- anisotropy;
- threshold onset;
- stability;
- hysteresis;
- propagation;
- transition cost;
- geometry-dependent response in controlled systems.

Remote reports may not be used to validate the mechanism. Visual resemblance does not establish shared dynamics.

A speculative application may be considered only after the relevant mechanism has been independently demonstrated and quantified in laboratory systems, and it must remain explicitly quarantined from the core evidential chain.

## 9. Metaphysical and ethical-language boundary

Love, cohesion, dignity, grace, God, soul, and related concepts may remain important philosophical, ethical, experiential, or systems-level parts of the wider project.

They must not be converted into unmeasured physical variables or used to rescue failed physical predictions.

Where cohesion is used quantitatively, it must be operationalised in domain-specific terms such as:

- correlation;
- alignment;
- contradiction reduction;
- stability;
- network integration;
- error correction;
- persistence under perturbation.

The ethical importance of a concept and its physical measurability are different questions.

## 10. Hard failure rules

A physics-facing MKUFT branch must be reduced, revised, or removed if:

- additional variables cannot be operationally defined;
- the equations add no predictive value beyond accepted models;
- the ordinary limit cannot be recovered;
- a claimed probability is non-normalisable or can become negative;
- dimensionful quantities enter exponentials without a declared scale;
- layer addresses cannot be distinguished operationally;
- the S-layer or O-layer can be removed without changing any prediction;
- a named mechanism remains only a placeholder after repeated revision;
- confirmatory predictions are selected after results are known;
- repeated adequately powered independent tests converge to the null;
- speculative examples are required to make the model appear predictive.

Failure of one branch must not be hidden inside the framework's breadth.

## 11. Reader-facing presentation order

For scientific readers, present MKUFT in this order:

1. exact claim and current status;
2. nearest established frameworks;
3. specific difference MKUFT proposes;
4. mathematical status label;
5. operational variables;
6. strongest ordinary null;
7. locked prediction or derivation target;
8. failure condition;
9. what success would and would not establish;
10. wider philosophical interpretation only after the evidence-bearing object is clear.

Do not lead with every linked domain. Breadth should appear as a map after one narrow mechanism has been secured.

## 12. Current honest scientific position

MKUFT is presently best described as:

> an original, broad, pressure-testable layered research architecture with disciplined mathematical scaffolds, explicit failure conditions, and several operational research programmes, but without a completed or independently validated new fundamental physical mechanism.

This is not a dismissal. It is the strongest defensible placement.

The route forward is not to make the claims louder. It is to make one mechanism narrower, measurable, derivable, and capable of losing cleanly.
