## 1. Purpose

MKUFT already separates reconnaissance, discriminating signatures, mechanism-level tests and foundational recovery. This note closes a narrower language seam: **failure to reach a higher evidential or promotion threshold must not be described as absence of evidence when lower-stage evidence actually exists.**

The reciprocal guard also remains mandatory: the existence of evidence must not be inflated into proof, mechanism identification, attribution or framework confirmation.

## 2. Evidence is not proof

At an explicitly addressed comparison between live hypotheses, a datum, observation, measurement, testimony, record, physical residue or lawful inference is evidentially relevant when it changes the relative support of those hypotheses. A schematic likelihood expression is:

```math
P(E\mid H_1) \neq P(E\mid H_0).
```

This is a comparison rule, not a declaration that every item labelled `E` is reliable or independent. Source quality, dependence, uncertainty, selection effects, measurement error and alternative explanations still determine evidential weight.

Keep these questions distinct:

1. Does evidence bearing on the addressed comparison exist?
2. What is its quality, uncertainty, independence and provenance?
3. How strongly does it discriminate the live alternatives?
4. Does it identify a mechanism rather than merely an anomaly or residual?
5. Does it support a broader attribution or ontology?
6. Has the relevant claim passed its declared promotion, replication or recovery gate?

A negative answer to questions 3–6 does not automatically make the answer to question 1 negative.

## 3. No zero-reset under a stronger gate

The following substitutions are invalid:

```text
not independently replicated → no evidence                 [INVALID]
not publicly inspectable      → no evidence                 [INVALID]
not decisive                  → no evidence                 [INVALID]
not mechanism-identifying     → no evidence                 [INVALID]
not Tier 2/3/4                → no evidence                 [INVALID]
not proven / established      → no evidence                 [INVALID]
```

Where evidence exists but a stronger burden is unmet, state the unmet burden directly. For example:

- evidence exists, but it is exploratory or weak;
- evidence exists, but it is dependent or not independently replicated;
- evidence exists, but it does not discriminate the strongest fair alternatives;
- evidence supports an anomaly/residual, but not a mechanism;
- evidence bears on a mechanism hypothesis, but is not sufficient for attribution;
- the result has not passed the required promotion or foundational-recovery gate.

Reserve `no evidence for H` for an addressed comparison where no carried observation, datum, record, measurement, testimony, residue or lawful inference has non-null relevance to `H` relative to the declared alternatives.

## 4. Promotion remains strict

This clarification does **not** lower the burden in Module 28.

A Tier 1 exploratory residual can be evidence while remaining inadequate for a Tier 2 theory-discriminating signature. A Tier 2 signature can support a local typed prediction while remaining inadequate for a Tier 3 mechanism claim. A Tier 3 mechanism result can remain inadequate for Tier 4 foundational recovery. Failure at a higher tier leaves the lower-stage result at the stage actually earned unless that lower-stage premise is itself falsified.

Likewise, a positive anomaly is not automatically an MKUFT result. The framework earns specific scientific support only where its predeclared variables, interactions, scaling rules, ablations, recovery conditions or other typed commitments discriminate it from the strongest fair competitors.

## 5. Strongest-fair-null relation

Module 24B asks the comparison to be strengthened until the serious fair null is genuinely competitive. Whatever survives that comparison is the evidence for the residual claim actually tested.

That principle has two symmetric protections:

```text
weak comparator must not manufacture evidence
strong promotion burden must not erase evidence already earned
```

When a stronger comparator explains the previous residual, the evidential state should update or collapse because the comparison changed. It should not collapse merely because proof, replication or ontology was never established in the first place.

## 6. Cross-scale and cross-claim carriage

Evidence must remain attached to the proposition and scale it actually bears on.

```text
local anomaly / residual evidence
↛ automatic mechanism evidence
↛ automatic ontology evidence
```

But also:

```text
mechanism not established
↛ local anomaly / residual evidence erased
```

Moving from observation → residual → structured signature → mechanism → broader framework or ontology is therefore a sequence of additional evidential burdens, not a sequence in which each unmet burden resets all previous evidence to zero.

## 7. Public-language guard

Scientific caution is best served by naming the exact epistemic state, not by using `no evidence` as shorthand for every state below proof.

Prefer:

> The observation is evidence for the local residual, but it is not independently replicated and does not yet discriminate the proposed mechanism from the strongest fair alternatives.

over:

> There is no evidence.

when the first sentence is the true state.

The inverse overstatement is equally prohibited. Evidence may be real while remaining weak, contaminated, non-exclusive or insufficient for promotion.

## 8. Falsification and reduction

This guard is itself falsifiable at the addressed comparison. If the evidence becomes irrelevant after correction, is shown to arise from artefact, loses the contrast that made it discriminating, or is absorbed by a stronger fair null, its weight may fall to zero or reverse.

The rule is therefore not `evidence once counted can never disappear`. It is:

> **Preserve the evidential stage actually earned until a reason relevant to that stage changes it. Do not substitute an unmet higher promotion gate for zero evidence, and do not substitute non-zero evidence for proof.**
