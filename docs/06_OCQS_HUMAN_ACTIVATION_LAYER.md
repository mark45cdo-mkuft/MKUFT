# OCQS — Optimal Cognitive Quiet State

**Author:** Mark Charles McLaughlin  
**Framework:** McLaughlin–Kairos Unified Field Theory (MKUFT)  
**MKUFT DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.17780566](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17780566)  
**Citation and provenance:** [PROVENANCE_DOI_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md](../PROVENANCE_DOI_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md)

**Status:** public observer-state support hypothesis.

## Purpose

OCQS names a candidate observer condition in which avoidable internal noise is reduced while attention, relevant emotional information, and discrimination remain available.

It is not passivity, infallibility, or evidence of nonlocal access.

The construct should be compared with established work on flow, attentional control, metacognition, predictive processing, embodied cognition, and performance under pressure.

## Functional role

OCQS is primarily an O-layer support hypothesis. It may provide measurable variables for studies of attention, working memory, error detection, confidence calibration, and stable performance.

Claims involving remote information, probability shifts, or substrate coupling remain separate hypotheses. OCQS does not establish them.

## Observer-side audit rule

A quieter observer is not automatically a more accurate observer. The public audit sequence is:

**observation → ordinary explanations → inference → uncertainty → falsifier → update.**

Reduced noise is useful only where accuracy, calibration, and reproducibility improve.

## Continuous variable

Let

$$
q_t\in[0,1]
$$

represent the measured degree to which preregistered quiet-state criteria are present. A candidate normalised model is

$$
\kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
q_t\,\kappa_{\mathrm{base}}\,\eta(F),
$$

with

$$
\eta(F)\in[0,1].
$$

This is not a physical law. Every study must define the measures, task, expected direction, null model, and ordinary causes of performance change.

## Layer placement

- **S:** no direct substrate claim.
- **I:** task structure and interpretation may influence the measured state.
- **P:** body, environment, instrumentation, and ordinary physical conditions constrain it.
- **O:** attention, record, confidence, and interpretation are the primary address.

OCQS cannot replace a missing P-layer mechanism.

## Predictions and falsifiers

A well-defined OCQS condition may correlate with lower performance variability, better error detection, improved confidence calibration, or more stable attention on selected tasks.

The hypothesis is weakened if independent measures cannot identify the state reliably, the proposed state predicts nothing beyond established constructs, performance gains do not reproduce, or confidence rises without accuracy.
