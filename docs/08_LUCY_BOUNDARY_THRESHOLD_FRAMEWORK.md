## Abstract

LUCY — **Local Unified Coherence Yield** — is MKUFT's threshold architecture for the transition from an unstable local gradient to a stable boundary and, where that boundary remains dynamically supported, to a sustained region whose behaviour differs from its exterior baseline.

The canonical sequence is **LUCY threshold → LUCY-1 membrane → LUCY-2 sustained pocket**.

The framework distinguishes **coherence** from the stronger condition of **relational closure**. Coherence means that active components or constraints are mutually consistent enough to reinforce a candidate state. Relational closure means that the relevant constraint loop is sufficiently complete for the state to persist, resist perturbation, and regulate crossings at its boundary.

LUCY does not claim that gravitation, inertia, light propagation, nuclear stability, cognition, or information routing are identical. It is a typed threshold architecture that may be addressed separately at informational, physical, and observer layers. Evidence cannot be inherited between those addresses without a defined carrier.

For physical applications, LUCY does not propose that a gravitational wave, particle, or force is amplified. Its stronger open hypothesis is that a local relational state may cross a threshold at which a different effective physical geometry becomes stable. Gravity-like observations would then be outputs of the selected geometry rather than the primitive object being generated.

The proposal overlaps established work on phase transitions, domain walls, emergent spacetime, induced gravity, analogue gravity, scalarisation, and collective states. Those topics are not claimed as original. The proposed MKUFT contribution is the typed conjunction of threshold, membrane, sustained pocket, relational closure, cross-channel boundary discrimination, strongest-fair-null testing, ordinary-physics recovery, and explicit falsification.

## 1. Exact object

LUCY means **Local Unified Coherence Yield**.

The exact proposal is:

> A declared local state crosses a prespecified threshold, forms a stable boundary, and may sustain a finite region with reproducibly different behaviour.

Every application requires an addressed layer, state variables, coherence or closure measure, control parameters, threshold, boundary condition, predicted post-threshold behaviour, ordinary baseline, and falsifier. Without those items LUCY remains descriptive language.

## 2. Three-stage sequence

### 2.1 LUCY — threshold

LUCY is the critical surface at which a candidate local state becomes dynamically admissible and stable enough to support boundary formation.

Below threshold, local alignment or fluctuation may occur without a stable maintained boundary. At threshold, the candidate state becomes self-reinforcing under declared conditions and may become a different stable or metastable solution.

### 2.2 LUCY-1 — membrane

A **LUCY membrane** is a stable interface separating two differently resolved local conditions. “Membrane” is functional language: the interface regulates crossing, coupling, routing, or state continuity. It does not require a biological membrane or literal sheet of matter.

A physical membrane claim should specify boundary thickness or resolution limit, lifetime, permeability or coupling rule, surface energy or maintenance cost, perturbation response, and observables locating the boundary.

### 2.3 LUCY-2 — sustained pocket

LUCY-2 is a finite region maintained by the LUCY-1 boundary. The preferred term is **sustained pocket** or **sustained local region**. “LUCY field” is appropriate only where a field is mathematically defined in the active domain.

A sustained pocket requires more than a momentary boundary. It must retain a distinguishable internal state for a declared time under declared noise, loading, and environmental conditions.

## 3. Coherence and relational closure

### 3.1 Coherence

Coherence is alignment or mutual consistency among the active constraints, phases, routes, or state variables relevant to the addressed system. Operational indicators may include reduced phase dispersion, reduced routing conflict, increased correlation, stable ordering, reduced transition noise, or persistent low-cost traversal.

### 3.2 Relational closure

Relational closure is achieved when the active relations form a sufficiently complete loop to maintain the state and regulate its boundary.

The candidate causal grammar is:

**state → boundary response → correction or reinforcement → retained state.**

Closure adds persistence, reciprocal constraint, perturbation correction, boundary maintenance, and a sufficiently complete action–response loop. The stronger physical LUCY claim begins at closure rather than resemblance, synchrony, or high correlation alone.

### 3.3 Deeper constraint-closure hypothesis

Coherence may be an observable consequence of a deeper transition in which a network of constraints becomes mutually satisfiable, reciprocally supporting, and sufficiently closed to persist under perturbation.

The proposed cross-domain grammar is:

**constraint compatibility → reciprocal support → closure threshold → coherent state → stable boundary → sustained local regime → domain-specific emergent behaviour.**

This does not assert that nuclear stability, collective physical phases, biological organisation, cognition, and effective geometry share one mechanism or equation. It proposes a common structural question: whether a previously unstable set of relations becomes self-supporting enough to maintain identity, boundary, correction, and continued operation.

The hypothesis is retained only if it adds predictive discrimination beyond established domain models. It fails as a physical addition when “constraint closure” merely redescribes stability after the fact, cannot be operationalised independently, or transfers mechanism between domains without a defined carrier.

### 3.4 O-layer closure address

Within the recursive MKUFT architecture, $O$ is the address at which a realised configuration becomes registered as a sufficiently determinate relation to complete a loop and become available for recurrence.

The source-level cycle is

$$
S\rightarrow I\rightarrow P\rightarrow O\rightarrow S.
$$

Applied locally to LUCY, the proposed sequence is **constraint compatibility at I → coherent realisation at P → registration and closure at O → boundary maintenance at P → sustained regime**.

$O$ is not restricted to a conscious human witness. Depending on the system, an observer-positioned registration function may be instantiated by measurement, internal feedback, boundary response, environmental coupling, or another durable state-recording process.

This is an architectural hypothesis, not a claim that human observation creates physical reality or that every measurement causes a LUCY transition. A developed model must identify the relevant registration channel and predict what changes when it is removed, delayed, corrupted, or decoupled.

## 4. Layer discipline

MKUFT uses the recursive typed architecture

$$
S\rightarrow I\rightarrow P\rightarrow O\rightarrow S.
$$

The layers are addresses rather than ordinary physical dimensions.

LUCY may be addressed as LUCY(I) for informational or routing constraint formation, LUCY(P) for physical realisation and boundary transition, and LUCY(O) for observer-positioned registration or closure where operationally defined.

A result at one address does not prove a result at another.

> No untyped inheritance.

An informational threshold is not evidence of a new physical field. An observer-state effect cannot become a material metric change without an independently measured P-layer carrier.

## 5. Established territory and proposed addition

LUCY does not claim novelty for phase transitions, critical thresholds, metastability, hysteresis, domain walls, interface energy, collective order parameters, symmetry breaking, nuclear shell or pairing effects, general relativity's geometric treatment of gravitation, or the emergence of several observables from one underlying state.

The framework overlaps research on induced and emergent gravity, thermodynamic gravitational dynamics, analogue gravity, many-body spacetime models, scalar-tensor phase transitions, spontaneous scalarisation, vacuum-state engineering, and nonlinear material or plasma boundaries.

The proposed MKUFT contribution is the specific conjunction of typed layer separation, threshold → membrane → pocket structure, relational closure, geometry or admissible-path state as the selected object rather than amplified force, common-boundary tests across independent observables, strongest-fair-null comparison, ordinary-physics recovery, and branch-level falsification.

## 6. Minimal threshold notation

A domain-specific LUCY model may define a yield index

$$
Y_L(x,t)
=
\chi_L
\frac{\lVert\nabla\tau_L(x,t)\rVert^2}
{N_L(x,t)+\varepsilon_L},
$$

with prespecified threshold

$$
Y_L(x,t)\ge Y_*.
$$

Here $\tau_L$ is a declared scalar gradient variable, $\chi_L$ a coupling or scale factor, $N_L$ a noise/disorder/competing-load measure, $\varepsilon_L>0$ a regulariser, and $Y_*$ the preregistered threshold.

This is a template rather than a universal law. It is valid only when the terms are defined, units or normalisation are consistent, and threshold crossing predicts a distinct post-threshold state.

Where curl-like behaviour is required, a vector potential must be introduced. A scalar $\tau_L$ cannot acquire a curl by notation alone.

## 7. State-selection scaffold

Let $q(x,t)$ be an order parameter and $\lambda$ a set of declared control parameters. A conventional effective functional is

$$
\mathcal F[q;\lambda]
=
\int_V
\left[
\frac{\kappa_q}{2}\lVert\nabla q\rVert^2
+V(q;\lambda)
\right]dV
+
\mathcal F_{\mathrm{boundary}}
+
\mathcal F_{\mathrm{environment}}.
$$

A toy potential may be

$$
V(q;\lambda)
=
\frac{a(\lambda)}{2}q^2
+
\frac{b}{4}q^4
+
\frac{c}{6}q^6,
\qquad c>0.
$$

The selected state is

$$
q_*
=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_q\mathcal F[q;\lambda].
$$

A LUCY critical surface $\Sigma_L$ may occur where competing preferred solutions exchange stability:

$$
\Delta\mathcal F(\lambda)
=
\mathcal F[q_1;\lambda]-\mathcal F[q_0;\lambda]
=0.
$$

This captures threshold, competing stable states, hysteresis, interface formation, and closure. It does not establish a new physical coupling.

## 8. Membrane and pocket energetics

For a roughly spherical physical pocket of radius $R$, a schematic nucleation cost is

$$
\Delta E(R)
=
4\pi R^2\sigma
-
\frac{4\pi}{3}R^3\Delta\varepsilon
+
E_{\mathrm{load}}(R)
+
E_{\mathrm{diss}}(R),
$$

where $\sigma$ is interface energy per unit area, $\Delta\varepsilon$ the bulk energy-density difference, and the final terms represent declared load and dissipation costs.

In the simplest uncoupled case,

$$
R_c=\frac{2\sigma}{\Delta\varepsilon}
$$

is the critical radius above which volume gain can exceed surface cost.

This is standard nucleation grammar used as a discipline: a sustained pocket must pay its boundary and maintenance costs. “Coherence” is not free energy.

## 9. Gravity-like behaviour as output

General relativity already models gravitation through spacetime geometry rather than an ordinary Newtonian force. LUCY does not claim that conceptual move as new.

The open proposal is:

> A local relational configuration may select or sustain an effective geometry. Matter paths, light paths, clock relations, and inertial behaviour would then be correlated outputs of that geometry.

The proposed chain is **constraint configuration → relational closure → selected effective geometry → matter/light/clock observables**.

It is not a claim that a small gravitational wave is amplified into a large gravitational force.

A possible scale relation between a local high-gradient regime and an extended effective-geometry regime would require an explicit typed map,

$$
C_{\ell\to L}:\mathcal X_{\ell}\rightarrow\mathcal X_L,
$$

showing how microscopic configuration becomes a macroscopic order parameter while preserving causality, energy accounting, and tested equivalence-principle limits. Until then the coupling may be zero.

## 10. Extreme configuration-sensitive matter

Neutron-rich superheavy nuclei are one candidate seed class because their stability can depend sharply on shell corrections, pairing, deformation, and fission barriers. Astrophysical rapid-neutron-capture environments may reach configurations unavailable in present terrestrial synthesis.

The permitted statement is that such environments may produce configuration-sensitive nuclei whose lifetimes differ substantially from measured neutron-deficient isotopes.

That does not imply altered gravitation. No accepted nuclear model supplies such a coupling, and within LUCY the coupling remains a hypothesis that may be absent.

A nucleosynthesis exposure parameter may be written

$$
\Theta_r
=
\tau_{\mathrm{env}}\,n_n\,\langle\sigma v\rangle.
$$

This describes neutron-capture exposure. It is not a geometry-changing parameter by analogy.

## 11. Geometry, scale, and threshold

Scaling can change category rather than merely magnitude, as in laser threshold, superconducting transition, critical nucleation, collective modes, and spontaneous ordering.

A valid scaling claim requires declared extensive and intensive variables, coherence length, finite-size corrections, geometry and boundary dependence, dissipation, critical exponent or explicit non-universal law, and ordinary limit.

A collective term proportional to $N^2$ cannot be inserted merely because coherent amplitudes sometimes add quadratically.

## 12. Discriminating physical signature

A serious P-layer LUCY claim requires several normally distinct observables to change at the same spatial boundary and control threshold.

Candidate channels include neutral test-mass motion, optical phase and path, clock comparison, fluid convection, electromagnetic propagation, thermal transport, material stress, pressure, and radiation or plasma diagnostics.

The load-bearing signature is:

> One boundary, one onset, one control threshold, and one model predicting the correlated residuals.

A change in only one channel normally favours a domain-specific explanation.

## 13. Required discriminators

### Threshold and hysteresis

The control parameter and onset are specified before observation. A sharp transition or hysteresis may support a phase-change model; smooth power-proportional response normally favours heating, leakage, or ordinary nonlinear response unless another mechanism is derived.

### Common boundary

Independent sensors must locate the same boundary within declared uncertainty.

### Geometry dependence

After controlling for cavity modes, stress, thermal transport, mass distribution, and fluid flow, normalised boundary geometry should predict threshold or effect class under a declared scaling relation.

### Composition and charge

A metric-like residual should couple approximately universally to neutral bodies. Strong dependence on charge, conductivity, magnetic susceptibility, dielectric constant, or surface finish favours conventional interactions.

### Wavelength

After plasma and material dispersion are removed, a metric-like optical-path residual should be approximately achromatic. Strong wavelength dependence favours refraction, absorption, scattering, plasma, or resonant material effects.

### Clocks and phase

At least two independent timing or phase methods should show a consistent residual before altered effective geometry is claimed.

### Conservation

Source energy, heat, radiation, momentum transfer, mechanical reaction, and entropy production remain part of the accounting.

### Astrophysical residue

If a particular extreme nuclear configuration is required, network calculations should predict distinctive abundance, decay-chain, or fission-fragment signatures. Failure to find the predicted signature as nuclear data improve weakens that seed branch.

## 14. Strongest fair null

Every physical test requires the strongest reasonable conventional explanation set, including electromagnetic, electrohydrodynamic, plasma, thermal, acoustic, mechanical, fluid-dynamic, optical, material, sensor/reconstruction, and relevant gravitational alternatives, including combined multifactor models.

The proposed LUCY model carries weight only where it predicts held-out observations better than the strongest fair null under matched information, calibration, and parameter freedom.

## 15. Evidence and promotion gates

### Tier 0 — mathematical consistency

Define spaces, variables, dimensions, update rule, stability, ordinary limit, and causality.

### Tier 1 — established analogue reproduction

Demonstrate threshold, membrane, and pocket behaviour in a known system without claiming new gravitation.

### Tier 2 — reproducible cross-channel residual

Under blinded and sham-controlled conditions, observe one mapped boundary in at least two independent channels.

### Tier 3 — effective-geometry discrimination

Show that one effective geometry predicts neutral matter, light, and clock behaviour better than the strongest conventional alternatives.

### Tier 4 — independent replication and scaling

A separate laboratory reproduces the effect and the preregistered geometry, threshold, composition, wavelength, and hysteresis predictions.

No foundational or ontology claim is promoted before these gates are met.

## 16. Ordinary-physics recovery

A developed LUCY model must recover ordinary behaviour when

$$
Y_L<Y_*,
$$

when the order parameter remains in the baseline minimum, the membrane is unstable, the pocket radius remains below critical size, the proposed cross-layer coupling is zero, or the measured residual falls below declared sensitivity.

In those regimes the addressed domain is described by its established ordinary theory.

A deeper physical LUCY account must also recover general relativity and quantum field theory within their tested domains.

## 17. Falsification and reduction

A LUCY branch is weakened, reduced, or rejected when the threshold cannot be operationally defined; the post-threshold state is not reproducibly distinct; no stable common boundary is located; different channels identify incompatible boundaries; effects scale with ordinary apparatus variables sufficient to explain them; the proposed geometry does not outperform the strongest conventional model; a dimensionally valid physical coupling cannot be constructed; conservation accounting fails; claimed scaling is fitted only after the result is known; notation changes meaning across layers; a proposed nuclear or material seed is unnecessary; held-out predictions fail; or the framework adds no discrimination beyond existing formalisms.

A failed physical branch is not rescued by relocating the effect to an unmeasured informational or observer layer.

## 18. What LUCY is not

LUCY is not a new force by declaration, gravity particle, gravity wave, universal field substance, synonym for consciousness, proof of extra dimensions, licence to treat analogy as mechanism, source of free energy, replacement for general relativity, replacement for quantum field theory, or label for every coherent system.

LUCY is a threshold architecture. Each physical application must earn its own mechanism and evidence.
