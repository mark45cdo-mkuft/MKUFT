## Purpose

This notice creates a public, independently timestamped record that a priority, provenance and attribution review has been raised concerning architectural overlap between the public MKUFT development lineage and material published by the Novakian Paradigm Institute under **Pre-Runtime Admissibility / Layer C**.

The purpose of this record is scholarly hygiene: to preserve the chronology, identify the objects that require comparison, and prevent later publication dates from being mistaken for the first appearance of every underlying MKUFT relation.

This notice does **not** state as an established fact that the Novakian Paradigm Institute copied MKUFT, had access to a particular MKUFT document, infringed copyright, or committed plagiarism. Those are separate evidential questions and are not inferred from chronology or architectural similarity alone.

## Object of comparison

The comparison is not based merely on shared vocabulary. It concerns the architecture and ordering of questions around, among other things:

- upstream state and constraint structure before downstream execution or law application;
- admissibility or permission-to-enter conditions before downstream operation or commitment;
- separation of architectural layers according to what each layer is entitled to determine;
- preservation of evidence, state, boundary and relational status before downstream inference;
- admissible state/future structure and constrained possibility;
- the possibility that runtime behaviour is a downstream observational surface rather than the correct first analytical address.

The relevant comparison must be performed against the **historical MKUFT development trail**, not only against the current August 2026 presentation or the dates of later standalone MKUFT papers. MKUFT develops iteratively in public repository states before later consolidation, audit and freezing into publication objects.

## Evidential boundary

The currently established record supports a request for **novelty, priority and provenance review**. It does not, by itself, establish transmission, access, copying, plagiarism or infringement.

Where a later public object overlaps an earlier independently timestamped MKUFT object, the chronology establishes public-expression order. Determining intellectual derivation requires additional evidence, including earlier versions, development records, search or citation histories, correspondence, repository history, or other evidence of access and transformation.

## Preservation and review

A formal preservation request has been made for records relevant to the development chronology of the overlapping material, including drafts, revision histories, repository states, research notes, AI-system interactions used materially in development, literature/web searches, and correspondence where relevant.
