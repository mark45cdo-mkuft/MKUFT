## What ARTA names

ARTA names the functioning integration of MKUFT's addressed-state, typed-relation, traversal, registration, readdressing and recursive-closure machinery as one scientific architecture.

At its most compressed:

```text
address the current object / state / target
→ preserve typed relations, boundaries and constraints
→ determine the lawful/admissible operation at that address
→ traverse / propagate / compare / intervene as the native domain permits
→ register the realised result through the declared evidence/readout surface
```

The exact implementation is domain-relative. ARTA does not assert that physics, biology, AI, cognition, institutions or other domains share one hidden mechanism merely because the same architecture can be instantiated across them.

## Why the four words are load-bearing

### Addressed

The architecture does not treat a statement, state or law as free-floating. The object is carried with the scale, boundary, target, context/history and registration conditions that are required for the declared operation. Readdressing is required when the realised transition changes what distinctions matter next.

### Relational

Component inventory is not automatically the functional object. Relations, constraints, interfaces, ordering, role and composition can carry load. Their contribution still has to survive the appropriate native controls, ablations, substitutions, translations and stronger simpler accounts.

### Traversal

The architecture is not a static taxonomy. It follows lawful change through states, representations, layers, scales, tests, measurements, interventions and restorative/reopening routes while preserving object identity, evidence status and return conditions.

### Architecture

ARTA is an organising scientific architecture, not one universal domain mechanism and not merely one software engine. Its value is tested by whether the integrated arrangement improves discrimination, prediction, state sufficiency, error localisation, recovery, transfer or other declared outcomes beyond strong native/domain baselines.

## Reduction / falsification boundary

ARTA earns scientific usefulness only to the extent that the integrated architecture does work.

The stronger ARTA-level claim contracts when, for a declared task and fair comparator:

- native/domain methods reproduce the same decisions, predictions, recovery or error localisation with equal or better performance and lower burden;
- removing or scrambling the claimed relational/traversal structure produces no material deformation;
- the same result follows from a materially simpler configuration;
- cross-domain use survives only by changing the meaning of its terms between domains;
- a local success is promoted into whole-architecture support without a demonstrated transfer relation;
- internal coherence or naming is mistaken for external evidence.

A null at one domain or task does not silently falsify every other independently tested ARTA instantiation. Equally, success in one domain does not automatically transfer mechanism or evidence to another.
