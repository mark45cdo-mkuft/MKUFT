# Recursive Exceptionalism / Epistemic Firewall — Public Route

**Live canonical module:** [Module 23A — Recursive Exceptionalism, Self-Sealing Authority Capture, and the Epistemic Firewall](docs/23A_RECURSIVE_EXCEPTIONALISM_SELF_SEALING_AUTHORITY_AND_EPISTEMIC_FIREWALL.md)

This route exposes a bounded applied-systems extension of [Module 23 — Agency Accessibility and Capture Geometry](docs/23_AGENCY_ACCESSIBILITY_AND_CAPTURE_GEOMETRY.md).

The core problem is recursive:

```text
important / high-salience event
→ special identity
→ detect and correct the special-identity error
→ make successful correction into evidence of unusual purity / humility / safety
→ special identity returns at a higher level
```

Module 23A treats this as a candidate measurable transfer problem rather than a moral slogan. It separates:

```text
experience / evidence
self-status
practical or epistemic authority
moral worth
```

and proposes prospective transfer diagnostics for:

```text
salience → status
status → authority
disconfirmation → confidence
humility / self-correction → renewed exceptionalism
```

It also provides a paired capture-pressure / guard-strength audit and a minimum experimental protocol.

## Why this is cross-domain

The relation does not depend on one spiritual, political, institutional, scientific, or AI ontology.

A common typed transfer can be asked in several native settings:

```text
SCIENCE
breakthrough / prestige salience
→ exceptional status
→ authority beyond demonstrated competence

AI
benchmark / self-audit success
→ "safe / superior" status
→ broader delegation than the tested task earned

LEADERSHIP / INSTITUTIONS
crisis success / rank
→ competence halo
→ authority imported into unrelated decisions

SPIRITUAL / ANOMALOUS EXPERIENCE
profound experience
→ chosen / cleaner status
→ wider epistemic or moral authority
```

These examples do **not** establish one shared causal mechanism. Each domain must define its own variables, evidence, strongest ordinary alternatives and failure conditions. What may survive across them is the typed relation: **salience or status must not silently manufacture authority at a different address.**

The public object therefore remains inspectable even if a reader brackets any one motivating domain. That is deliberate. A cross-domain architecture should not require acceptance of its most culturally expensive example before the relation can be tested elsewhere.

For the domain-native replacement rule, use the [Professional Domain Instantiation Guide](PROFESSIONAL_DOMAIN_INSTANTIATION_GUIDE.md). For the public carrier rule, use [Public Writing — Metaphor and Cognitive-Load Rule](PUBLIC_WRITING_METAPHOR_AND_COGNITIVE_LOAD_RULE.md).

> **Sober presentation does not cool the relation. It removes only non-load-bearing ontology, identity or prestige from the admission path so that the native relation, evidence and falsifier reach inspection.**

## Boundary

This is not a clinical scale, purity score, cult detector, deception detector, or proof of any spiritual/paranormal ontology. It is a falsifiable systems proposal for detecting wrong-address status and authority transfer while preserving genuine evidence, expertise, dignity and meaningful experience.

A cross-domain rhyme does not prove mechanism identity. If the mapped relation cannot be operationalised independently in the receiving domains, adds no predictive/discriminating value beyond established constructs, or survives only by changing the meaning of its variables after the outcome is known, the cross-domain claim contracts or fails.
