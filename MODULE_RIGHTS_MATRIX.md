1. **Copyright ownership remains with Mark Charles McLaughlin unless a signed written assignment states otherwise.**

3. **A DOI is a provenance and citation identifier, not a licence.** A reference to the MKUFT DOI does not import the licence of a different deposited work.
4. **Rights attach to the exact material and version being used.** A licence attached to a frozen deposit applies to that deposited material; it does not automatically license later GitHub revisions or separate publications.
5. **Historical grants remain effective for the exact material licensed.** Nothing in this repository purports to revoke permissions already granted under CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
6. **Current-version reservation does not recapture historical Creative Commons material.** Historical material remains available from the deposit under the licence attached to that deposit. New expression, arrangement, additions, revisions, and current-version compilation are not automatically covered by an older licence.
7. **Where no exact file, publication, or version states a licence, the current-version default is all rights reserved:** Copyright © 2026 Mark Charles McLaughlin. All rights reserved.
8. **No repository-level software licence is granted.** Publication of manuscripts, specifications, hypotheses, equations, or protocols does not grant a software, source-code, patent, trademark, certification, or commercial-implementation licence.
9. **Copyright boundary:** these notices govern copyright-protected expression and any other rights actually held. They do not claim copyright ownership of facts, abstract ideas, scientific principles, mathematical relationships, methods, functionality, or independently written code as such, and they do not create patent rights.

## Practical reading rule

For any reuse, identify the exact object first:

1. the exact file or frozen deposit;
2. its version or commit;
3. any licence stated inside that exact object;
4. its specific DOI record, if any;
5. this matrix and [Rights and Licence Notice](RIGHTS_AND_LICENSE_NOTICE.md) for the current repository position.

Where those sources differ, the licence attached to the exact material being used controls. A general provenance reference cannot broaden that licence.
