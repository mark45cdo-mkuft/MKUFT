## Transition-carriage corollary

The same construction has an immediate traversal consequence already implicit in AAF's recursive readdressing:

```math
A_t
\rightarrow
\mathcal F_t^+
\rightarrow
\tau_t
\rightarrow
A_{t+1}
\rightarrow
\mathcal F_{t+1}^+.
```

A realised transition may change the effective Address and therefore the admissible future. A continuation projected at $A_t$ retains standing after the transition only where its remaining suffix is still admissible from the readdressed state:

```math
\gamma_{t,\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{proj}}
\in
\mathcal F^+(A_{t+1}).
```

Where that condition fails, continuation is recomputed from $A_{t+1}$ rather than inherited from the earlier projection. The state/history carrier need preserve only those distinctions whose removal would merge histories with materially different declared futures; unlimited history is not implied.

This is an explicit corollary of the existing future-sufficiency and recursive-readdressing construction, not a third named principle, a new physical law, or a claim that precomputed trajectories are generally invalid. In regimes where the transition leaves the relevant Address and future geometry unchanged, the corollary is null and the projected continuation remains admissible.

## Observer-bounded traversal corollary

The future-sufficiency family now carries one further bounded consequence without changing its scientific status.

Let `O_t` be the declared observer/registration state and let `W_t^q` denote only the retained trajectory residue that remains decision-bearing for target `q`. Let

```math
\mathcal D_t^q
=
\mathcal D(A_t,O_t,W_t^q,E_t,q)
```

schematically denote the lawful discriminator family currently reachable from the addressed state. A future-splitting defect inside the current representation creates a **distinction-recovery burden**, but does not privilege verbal questioning. A discriminator may be a measurement, comparison, perturbation, representation change, scale move, retrieval, controlled wait, or another lawful domain-native operation.

A realised discriminator may change the state from which later discrimination is possible:

```math
(A_t,O_t,W_t^q)
\xrightarrow{d_t}
(A_{t+1},O_{t+1},W_{t+1}^q),
```

so that

```math
\mathcal D_{t+1}^q
\neq
\mathcal D_t^q
```

can be legitimate. This does not imply that a later question or measurement was secretly available earlier. It says only that traversal may change the address, evidence, representation, instrumentation, or access needed to formulate or execute it.

History is screened back to provenance where the present typed state already mediates its target-relevant future effect. The route therefore preserves **future-bearing wake**, not history for history's sake.
