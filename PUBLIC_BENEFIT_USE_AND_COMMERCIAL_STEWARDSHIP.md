## A humble note before the legal detail

This work is public because it is meant to be **read, tested, criticised, taught, improved, and — where it genuinely helps — used**.

It is not published as a legal trap for a student, independent researcher, teacher, scientist, charity, volunteer group, public-interest organisation, community project, public body, or individual builder acting in good faith.

If your purpose is to understand the work, challenge it, reproduce an experiment, teach it, cite it, test it, or independently implement its ideas and methods for non-commercial research, safety, accessibility, humanitarian, educational, community, or other genuine public benefit, **you are on the intended path**.

Please preserve attribution and provenance, mark your own changes honestly, and do not imply endorsement, certification, or official status that has not been granted.

The project posture toward good-faith public-benefit use is **stewardship and collaboration, not intimidation**.

## The two routes are deliberate

MKUFT already has two compatible public entry routes:

1. the **Public Entry**, for a reader who needs the object and its boundaries first;
2. the **Scientific Reader Traversal**, for a reader who wants to enter the technical body without reconstructing the whole development history.

This note applies at both entry routes.

The rights structure also has two compatible use routes:

### Route A — public-benefit / non-commercial use

Use the exact material according to the licence attached to that exact object.

You do **not** need special project permission merely to:

- read, link to, cite, criticise, discuss, or test the work where ordinary law allows;
- exercise rights already granted by an exact Creative Commons licence;
- reproduce experiments or compare scientific claims using your own implementation and ordinary lawful research practice;
- independently implement facts, abstract ideas, scientific principles, mathematical relationships, methods, functionality, or other material that copyright does not monopolise;
- write your own code implementing an idea or method, provided you are not copying protected source code or other protected expression.

Where a current live repository object is **all rights reserved** and a genuine non-commercial public-benefit project needs permission to reproduce, adapt, translate, or redistribute protected text, diagrams, tables, or other expression beyond what applicable law already permits, the project's standing policy is a **presumption in favour of no-fee written permission** where Mark Charles McLaughlin has the rights to grant it and the use:

- is genuinely non-commercial and not primarily directed toward commercial advantage;
- preserves reasonable attribution and provenance;
- clearly marks adaptations or material changes;
- does not imply endorsement, certification, affiliation, or official status;
- does not conflict with third-party rights, law, safety obligations, or an existing licence condition.

That policy is intended to remove unnecessary fear for people genuinely trying to learn, test, teach, help, or build for public benefit.

It is **not** a silent relicensing of every all-rights-reserved file. Where written permission is legally required for a reserved object, obtain that written permission so the scope is clear.

### Route B — commercial / proprietary / extractive use

A separate written commercial permission route applies where the use of protected project material is primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation, including where applicable:

- proprietary product or service development;
- incorporation of protected text, diagrams, documentation, datasets, or software into a paid or proprietary offering;
- paid consulting or commercial implementation;
- proprietary model, agent, benchmark, training, evaluation, or optimisation pipelines using protected material where permission is legally required;
- internal commercial R&D or competitive capability development using protected material in a way that requires permission;
- certification, accreditation, or assurance activity presented as official or project-authorised;
- sublicensing, resale, or commercial redistribution of protected material.

Commercial use is **not morally condemned by this distinction**. It simply uses a different route because commercial extraction and public-benefit sharing are not the same rights object.

Commercial users are welcome to seek permission, partnership, or a licence.

## This is not a company-size test

The boundary is the **use**, not the logo on the door.

A small company building a proprietary paid product is still making a commercial use.

A large university, public body, foundation, or company may in some circumstances conduct work that is genuinely non-commercial and public-interest in character.

Do not infer permission from organisation size. Apply the exact material's licence and the actual use.

For Creative Commons material using the NonCommercial condition, the controlling CC definition is whether the use is primarily intended for or directed toward **commercial advantage or monetary compensation**.

## Copyright is not ownership of ideas

The project does not claim copyright monopoly over:

- facts;
- abstract ideas;
- scientific principles;
- mathematical relationships;
- methods;
- functionality;
- independently written code as such.

Copyright can protect original expression such as text, diagrams, tables, code, and original selection or arrangement to the extent the law protects them. It does not turn a scientific or architectural idea into private property merely because the idea is described in a copyrighted document.

This boundary matters in both directions:

> **Public-benefit users should not be frightened away from lawful independent use of ideas. Commercial actors should not confuse the limits of copyright with permission to copy protected expression, software, branding, or confidential implementation material.**

Separate patent, trademark, trade-secret, contractual, certification, database, software, or other rights may have their own rules. This note does not create rights that do not otherwise exist.

## Software is separate

Creative Commons licences are primarily content licences and are not the default software licence for this project.

No repository-level software licence is granted unless an exact software object expressly says otherwise.

Independent implementation of an unprotected idea or method is different from copying source code.

## Attribution and provenance

Where attribution is legally required by an exact licence, follow that licence.

Even where the law does not require it, the project asks good-faith users to preserve enough provenance that another reader can tell:

- what came from MKUFT / ARTA / ATLD;
- what version or DOI was used;
- what you changed;
- what conclusions are yours;
- whether your implementation or interpretation is official, unofficial, independent, or derivative.

Citation is not tribute. It is part of the memory system of research.

## No endorsement or certification by implication

Permission to read, use, test, implement, cite, or adapt material does not by itself mean that Mark Charles McLaughlin, MKUFT, ARTA, ATLD, Kairos, or Custodian Systems endorses the resulting project, product, paper, model, service, interpretation, or claim.

Official certification, accreditation, partnership, endorsement, trademark use, or commercial representation requires separate explicit authority.

## The practical rule

If you are here to **learn, test, teach, investigate, reproduce, or genuinely help without extracting commercial advantage**, the project's intended posture is:

> **Use the work responsibly. Preserve provenance. Do not be afraid of the architecture.**

If you are here to turn protected project material into proprietary commercial advantage:

> **Use the commercial permission route.**

If you are unsure which exact rights apply, identify the exact object/version first and check the [Rights and Licence Notice](RIGHTS_AND_LICENSE_NOTICE.md) and [Module Rights Matrix](MODULE_RIGHTS_MATRIX.md).

## Legal-status boundary

This note is a canonical statement of project stewardship policy and intended routing.

It does not:

- revoke or narrow an existing Creative Commons licence;
- silently relicense every current all-rights-reserved repository object;
- create a software, patent, trademark, certification, or commercial-implementation licence;
- override applicable law or third-party rights;
- claim copyright control over ideas, methods, facts, or mathematical relationships.

Where an exact licence or written permission is legally required, that exact licence or permission controls.
