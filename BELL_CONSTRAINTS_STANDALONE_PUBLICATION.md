## What the paper establishes

The paper keeps several Bell/CHSH statements at separate scientific addresses rather than compressing them into one claim. In particular, it separates:

- Bell-local hidden-variable factorisation;
- operational no-signalling of observed marginals;
- a stronger history-conditioned process-closure condition;
- local admissibility/uncertainty ownership;
- parent-level completion geometry;
- scenario scale from internal resource/capacity scale;
- algebraic-target residual from quantum-bound slack, finite-address excess, certification gap, and experimental uncertainty.

The central typed distinction is:

> **relation ≠ operational access ≠ remote write-power ≠ process closure ≠ local admissibility ownership ≠ parent completion.**

Established Bell and quantum-information mathematics remains native owner wherever it already supplies the result. The paper therefore treats Fine's theorem, no-signalling, Tsirelson–Landau–Masanes correlator geometry, Information Causality, NPA, incompatibility/joint measurability, uncertainty/steering, relativistic independence, dimension witnesses, and neighbouring stochastic-process reformulations as comparators rather than novelty credit.

## Claim boundary

The paper does **not** claim:

- a new Bell inequality;
- a new derivation of Tsirelson's bound from an independent physical principle;
- that Bell's theorem has been solved;
- that the CHSH algebraic-to-quantum residual is a new substance, universal imperfection constant, or causal law;
- that a change of Bell scenario is by itself an increase of internal capacity;
- that larger ambient Hilbert-space dimension proves irreducibly larger required dimension.

Under the comparator family examined in v1.0, the **independent Bell-local new-physics delta is null**. The non-null contribution is the typed correspondence, custody, scale-discipline, and falsification architecture, including a prospective reopening rule that requires a genuinely held-out separator and survival against the strongest relevant native owner.
