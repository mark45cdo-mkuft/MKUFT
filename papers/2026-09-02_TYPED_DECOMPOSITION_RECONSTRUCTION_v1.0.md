## Abstract

Scientific models can fail because a representation has grouped together states, relations, scales, histories, or properties that are equivalent for one purpose but not for the target currently being predicted, controlled, or explained. This paper formalises **Typed Decomposition-Reconstruction (TDR)** as a prospective protocol for detecting and testing such hidden scientific conflations.

TDR starts from a native representation and declared target. When materially different target futures, interventions, or closure decisions remain inside one operational address, the method decomposes only the decision-bearing object, types the resulting components and relations, forbids automatic property inheritance across unlicensed relations, reconstructs only through explicit carrier or realisation relations, subtracts native and simpler ownership, and requires the proposed repair to improve held-out or prospective discrimination.

The paper does **not** claim novelty for decomposition, abstraction, typing, causal abstraction, predictive state, sufficient representation, ablation, model selection, or hidden-state search individually. The narrower candidate is the ordered conjunction and its prospective accountability rule.

A secondary construct, **Wake**, is defined as a target-relative retained traversal/history variable: the smallest load-bearing residue of prior traversal that changes present interpretation, prediction, or admissible continuation. Wake is not a substance, universal memory law, or replacement for domain-native hysteresis.

Two already-public source-programme outcomes are included as **retrospective calibrations of verdict quality**, not as prospective evidence for TDR superiority. ATLD 2 supplies a positive diagnostic/no-coordinate-inflation case. The Bell/CHSH typed-boundary analysis supplies a productive physical null in which the independent new-physics delta remained null while a non-null typed correspondence and falsification result survived.

## Why the method is worth testing

The performance claim is deliberately unawarded, but its significance is not trivial. If independent teams can freeze TDR before seeing outcomes and then use it to identify decision-bearing distinctions that strong native or generic methods miss, TDR would amount to a **transferable discovery instrument**: not a new law of nature, but a reproducible way of finding where a representation has hidden a consequential distinction.

That possibility should neither be promoted in advance nor cooled into “mere housekeeping.” It belongs under a benchmark capable of separating those outcomes.

## Target-relative hidden conflation

Let

```math
A_{\Theta}:\mathcal X\rightarrow\mathcal Z_{\Theta}
```

be a native operational address. States are equivalent at that address when

```math
x\sim_{\Theta}x'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A_{\Theta}(x)=A_{\Theta}(x').
```

For target `q`, horizon `Δ`, intervention class `U`, environment class `E`, transition kernel `K^Δ`, and discrepancy `d_Q`, define the target-relative address residual

```math
\mathcal R_q(\Theta;U,E,\Delta)
=
\sup_{\substack{x\sim_{\Theta}x'\\u\in U,\ e\in E}}
 d_Q\!\left(
 q_*K^{\Delta}(\cdot\mid x,u,e),
 q_*K^{\Delta}(\cdot\mid x',u,e)
 \right).
```

The representation closes for the declared target when

```math
\boxed{
\mathcal R_q(\Theta;U,E,\Delta)
\leq
\varepsilon_q.
}
```

A residual above tolerance is a diagnostic trigger, not proof of one preferred hidden variable or ontology.

For a candidate coordinate `c`, let

```math
\Theta^+=\Theta\cup\{c\}
```

and define completion gain

```math
\Gamma_q(c\mid\Theta)
=
\mathcal R_q(\Theta;U,E,\Delta)
-
\mathcal R_q(\Theta^+;U,E,\Delta).
```

Retention requires protected gain plus native-owner, leakage, complexity, and simpler-state subtraction.

## Three domain mappings

The paper works the same procedural burden through three different domains without claiming that those domains share one mechanism.

### Physics

Informational adjacency and physical proximity are separated:

```math
\mathrm{adjacent}_{I}(X,Y)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathrm{near}_{P}(X,Y),
```

```math
\mathrm{near}_{P}(X,Y)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathrm{adjacent}_{I}(X,Y).
```

A physical consequence still requires a declared realisation/coupling with native variables, units or normalisation where applicable, baseline and falsifier. The distinction may be conceptually useful without inheriting causal authority.

### Biology

Two cells may share the same present operational address while retaining different protected futures. TDR asks whether a native variable such as stress history, regulatory state, lineage, microenvironment, metabolic state, epigenetic state, cell-cycle state, measurement aliasing, or model error carries the split. A history coordinate earns state status only if it reduces the target residual prospectively beyond simpler variables.

### AI evaluation

A candidate residual coordinate with positive diagnostic gain is not automatically independent. The existing measurement body must first be tested for direct ownership, derived ownership and coalition recovery. A correct result may be an interaction term or improved scoring rule rather than a new dimension.

## Two already-executed calibrations: success without inflation

These cases are historical calibrations because they predate the frozen standalone TDR protocol. They do **not** count as prospective evidence that TDR beats comparator methods. They do show why outcome quality cannot be collapsed into “new thing discovered” versus “failure.”

### ATLD 2: positive diagnostic result without coordinate inflation

ATLD 2 used a 15-case exploratory Release Relay execution pilot. On RR-14, the foreign-system trace exposed a branch-localisation / route-support / calibration seam. The seam was real enough to localise, but existing ATLD 2 machinery already owned it: the branch-localisation coordinate together with route-support / first-break machinery and the evidence-path readout carried the residual. A thirteenth coordinate was therefore **not** justified.

In TDR terms:

```math
\text{material residual}
\rightarrow\text{candidate distinction}
\rightarrow\text{existing-owner subtraction}
\rightarrow\text{diagnostic localisation}
\rightarrow\text{REDUCE / MERGE rather than invent}.
```

That is a **positive methodological calibration**: the instrument found a real seam and correctly refused to convert that success into unnecessary ontology. It does not establish empirical superiority of the private structured condition or validate every candidate ATLD 2 coordinate.

### Bell/CHSH: productive physical null, non-null typed result

The Bell typed-boundary analysis separated Bell-local factorisation, operational no-signalling, remote write-power, history-conditioned process closure, local admissibility ownership, parent completion, and scale/resource custody, then compared those objects against established native Bell and quantum owners.

Under the tested comparator family, the independent Bell-local **new-physics delta was null**. The typed correspondence, custody and falsification architecture remained **non-null**.

The TDR verdict is therefore typed:

```math
\begin{aligned}
&\text{PRESERVE: typed relation/access/completion/scale distinctions},\\
&\text{REDUCE: known numerical Bell boundaries to native owners},\\
&\text{REJECT / CLOSE: independent Bell-local new-physics claim at the tested scope}.
\end{aligned}
```

That is a productive null. The stronger physical novelty claim closes while the lower-level methodological result survives.

## Outcome salience is not evidence inflation

This pre-publication revision closes a specific reporting hazard. Scientific restraint is **not** achieved by stating only the most deflationary sentence. A positive result stated above its evidence scope is inflation; a positive result omitted because it sounds impressive is also a measurement error.

TDR therefore keeps **outcome importance** and **evidence authority** separately typed. A correctly earned positive may be stated positively at its actual scope. A null remains null at the scope where it is null. Neither is allowed to erase the other.

A method that can announce only its losses is as badly calibrated as one that calls every repair a win.

## Wake and Wake Gap

Let available traversal history be `H_{0:t}` and a candidate retained compression be

```math
W_t=\Phi(H_{0:t}).
```

The augmented state is

```math
S_t^+=(S_t,W_t).
```

Wake earns load only when it improves the declared target beyond the simpler present-state representation. A predictive version is

```math
\Gamma_q^{W}
=
\mathcal R_q(\Theta;U,E,\Delta)
-
\mathcal R_q(\Theta\cup\{W\};U,E,\Delta).
```

A **Wake Gap** is a target-relative difference in retained traversal state that produces materially different interpretation, prediction, or lawful continuation under the same present input. If present state already closes the target, Wake returns null.

## Prospective generator test

For prospectively frozen task `q`, let `\mathcal G_{\mathrm{TDR}}` be TDR, `\mathcal B_q` a preregistered family of serious comparators, and `F_q(M)` a higher-is-better portfolio score under matched information, time, compute, human assistance, search budget, revision opportunity, and stopping rule.

The score must include false positives, failed branches, repairs, search cost and revisions. It must also reward **correct positive retention and correct reduction/rejection/abstention** against protected outcomes. Rewarding only novelty biases the benchmark toward over-splitting; rewarding only conservative rejection recreates the cooling failure.

Define the strongest comparator:

```math
F_{\mathrm{base}}^{*}(q)
=
\sup_{b\in\mathcal B_q}F_q(b).
```

Then define

```math
\boxed{
G_{\mathrm{gen}}(q)
=
F_q(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{TDR}})
-
F_{\mathrm{base}}^{*}(q).
}
```

Interpretation:

```text
G_gen(q) > 0  → TDR wins on that frozen task/envelope
G_gen(q) ≈ 0  → no demonstrated advantage
G_gen(q) < 0  → strongest comparator wins
```

A negative value is a real loss for TDR, not a hidden confirmation. Equally, a correctly earned positive result need not contain a new mechanism or coordinate: **correctly scoped verdict quality**, not novelty count, is the benchmark object.

## Scientific status

The equations above are methodological/statistical scaffolds until the task family, comparators, score, resource envelope, stopping rule, uncertainty model, and protected outcomes are frozen and run. They are not physical laws.

The broad TDR claim should reduce or fail if the method is hindsight-dependent, unstable across independent analysts, no better than native methods, complexity-inflating, unable to abstain, dependent on privileged resource advantages, or consistently null/negative under `G_gen`.
