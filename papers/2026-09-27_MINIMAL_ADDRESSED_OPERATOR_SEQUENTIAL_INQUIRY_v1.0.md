## Abstract

This preprint asks whether, after domain-specific objectives, mechanisms, and representations are stripped away, a clean sequential inquiry process admits a representation-independent addressed factorisation.

Extracted from the published MKUFT/ATLD/ARTA lineage, the candidate contract advances from a target-indexed addressed state through a lawful target-relevant inquiry move, a registered realised outcome, and mandatory readdressing before the next question is posed. Minimal query selection is treated separately as a prospective refinement under an independently fixed burden order, while recursive scale handoff is kept as a distinct conditional construction.

The paper states seven clean-inquiry requirements, formulates a general factorisation conjecture, develops prospective continuation-kernel conditions for minimal replacement, and supplies explicit reduction and falsification tests against strong neighbouring formalisms including dynamic logics of questions, Blackwell comparison of experiments, Bayesian experimental design, rational metareasoning, POMDPs, active learning, active inference, predictive-state methods, canonical history quotients, minimal sufficient information transition systems, and compositional systems.

No universal minimal-inquiry theorem is claimed. This v1.0 preprint freezes the addressed inquiry contract, factorisation conjecture, prospective minimal-replacement problem, recursive-scale handoff, strongest nulls, and falsification programme as a stable citable research object.

## Formal core

The general candidate is

\[
I_n=(A_n,T_n)
\xrightarrow{\;q_n\in\mathcal Q^{\dagger}(A_n,T_n)\;}
O_n\sim K_O(\cdot\mid A_n,T_n,q_n)
\xrightarrow{\;o_n\;}
I_{n+1}=\mathcal R_I(A_n,T_n,q_n,o_n).
\]

Here:

- \(A_n\) is the current addressed state;
- \(T_n\) is the declared target;
- \(q_n\) is a lawful target-relevant inquiry move;
- \(O_n\) is the prospective stochastic outcome variable;
- \(o_n\) is the registered realised outcome;
- \(\mathcal R_I\) returns the post-change inquiry state, including any target persistence, refinement, closure, or replacement.

The general factorisation does **not** require locally minimal query choice.

A minimal representative is a separate refinement problem. Where a prospectively fixed burden order is informative and replacement conditions are earned, the candidate minimal set is

\[
\mathcal Q_{\min}(A,T)=
\operatorname{Min}_{\preceq_{A,T}}\mathcal Q^\dagger(A,T).
\]

If that set is empty, no minimum is earned.

## Prospective replacement

Replacement cannot be justified by looking at the outcome after one branch has already been selected. For a common target-relevant continuation-class map \(C_T\), each lawful query induces a prospective continuation kernel

\[
\Pi_q^{A,T}
=
\left(C_T\circ\mathcal R_I(A,T,q,\cdot)\right)_{\#}
K_O(\cdot\mid A,T,q).
\]

Minimal replacement is therefore tested before outcome reveal, against a prospectively declared discrepancy/tolerance and burden order. Where an established native order such as Blackwell informativeness already settles the comparison, that result remains owned by the native theory rather than being renamed as MKUFT minimality.

## Clean-inquiry contract

The paper tests whether a representation-independent implementation can preserve seven semantic obligations without importing MKUFT-specific nouns by definition:

1. **object/context fidelity**;
2. **target-relative relevance**;
3. **lawful query/intervention** at the current inquiry context/state;
4. **outcome/evidence custody**;
5. **realised-state update** rather than projected-state substitution;
6. **post-change state fidelity**;
7. **recursive continuation** from the state actually reached.

MKUFT's typed Address and readdressing are candidate realisations of those obligations. A valid factorisation must construct those roles from the comparator semantics; it cannot assume that the compared system already contains variables or operations named \`Address\` or \`readdressing\`.

## Enabling moves

Target relevance is broader than immediate discrimination. Calibration, restoration, representation change, access acquisition, or another preparatory move may be lawful and target-relevant if it prospectively changes which target-relevant discriminator or closure route becomes reachable.

To prevent an unbounded “might help eventually” escape hatch, the paper requires a finite positive horizon, a concrete downstream witness, a comparator route, and—in stochastic cases—a strictly positive material-probability threshold under which the witness becomes reachable.

## Recursive scale handoff

The local inquiry operator and the scale claim are kept separate.

A local inquiry step does not imply scale promotion. Where a local whole is independently shown to be closed for the declared property and scope, a typed promotion map may allow that whole to become a constituent in a wider relational bracket. An analogous inquiry grammar may then recur at the new Address.

That is a structural recurrence claim, not a claim that the same physical mechanism operates at every scale.

## Strongest fair nulls

The paper is designed to contract cleanly if stronger ordinary formalisms already recover the full semantic burden.

Major nulls include:

- state augmentation absorbs the entire contract into an ordinary state-transition formalism;
- Bayesian experimental design or Blackwell comparison recovers the query-selection/replacement burden;
- a POMDP or predictive-state representation recovers the required state/update semantics;
- active learning or rational metareasoning recovers the inquiry/computation-selection burden;
- canonical history quotients or minimal sufficient information transition systems recover the full task-sufficient state without a residual;
- compositional/open-system mathematics recovers the scale/interface structure;
- representation changes preserve the same semantics without requiring a distinct addressed factorisation.

A reduction is a valid scientific result. The paper does not survive merely because its vocabulary is different.

The current ARTA engine already executes the operational sequence:

\[
\text{bind}

\rightarrow
\text{address}
\rightarrow
\text{prospect}
\rightarrow
\text{select lawful route}
\rightarrow
\text{execute}
\rightarrow
\text{observe}
\rightarrow
\text{readdress}
\rightarrow
\text{learn/continue}.
\]

The paper therefore serves as a **compression and conformance research object**, not a new controller. If the factorisation programme succeeds, it may provide a compact implementation-neutral contract against which different engines can be tested. If it fails, the engine remains owned by its existing modules and the paper contracts to a synthesis/reduction result.
