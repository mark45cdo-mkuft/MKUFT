# 10. Cross-scale law descent, closure, and recoverability

A higher-order object does not automatically own an autonomous law.
MKUFT separates constitutive closure, property-specific law sufficiency,
and recoverability because these boundaries can fail at different
points.

- Constitutive closure asks whether the relation that makes the whole
  the declared kind of whole persists or restores under perturbation.

- Law sufficiency asks whether a declared target property can be
  propagated at the higher address without material unresolved
  lower-state dependence.

- Recoverability asks whether an admissible route can return the system
  to a declared target closure class under declared controls,
  environment, route constraints, and horizon.

Readdressing is therefore bidirectional: lower address <->
higher/effective address <-> coupled multiscale address. Evidence may
force the active description upward, downward, or into a coupled
multiscale state without denying that a higher-level functional object
can remain real.

# 11. Prior art and residual contribution after subtraction

MKUFT does not claim priority over measure spaces, Hilbert spaces,

# 12. Falsification, contraction, and framework-level reduction

A broad architecture is scientifically useful only if breadth cannot
protect a failed branch. MKUFT therefore allows both local and
framework-level reduction.

## 12.1 Branch-level rejection conditions

- Variables cannot be operationally defined.
- A comparison across changed addresses lacks a lawful map.
- A proposed probability is negative or non-normalisable.
- A dimensionful quantity enters an exponential without a declared reciprocal scale.
- The accepted ordinary physical limit cannot be recovered.
- The strongest fair null predicts equally well or better.
- A proposed load-bearing relation can be ablated, substituted, or scrambled without the predicted deformation.
- Held-out predictions fail or independent replications converge to the null.
- Post-hoc flexibility is required to preserve the claim.
- Evidence from one layer is used to rescue failure at another.
- A claimed scale transition is only analyst coarse-graining with no endogenous change in accessible relations.
- A recovery or irreversibility claim lacks a target class, controls, route constraints, environment, or horizon.

## 12.2 Framework-level reduction

If no proposed cross-layer variable adds independent content, MKUFT may
reduce to a typed methodology for state representation, model selection,
and cross-scale auditing. If the capstone cannot be instantiated
consistently, even that architectural claim must be narrowed. If quantum
and gravitational recovery fail, the foundational unified-field claim
fails regardless of successes in non-foundational applications. These
are contractions, not rescue clauses.

# 13. Discussion

The principal scientific claim of this synthesis is methodological
before it is ontological. Modern foundational work can fail not only
because an equation is numerically wrong, but because the object being
evolved, compared, compressed, or promoted has silently changed type or
address. The immediate stress tests make that claim concrete in three
different evidential forms: a standard Bell point can be calculated
through the chart and then stripped of false novelty; a future split can
expose a state representation that became insufficient only after
widened reuse; and a matched-control AI experiment can ask whether
relation meaning and recursive traversal themselves carry functional
load. MKUFT attempts to make those transitions explicit enough that they
can be attacked rather than protected by vocabulary.

This matters particularly under recursive modelling. A local
representation can be correct for the task that created it and still
become inadequate when reused inside a wider composition. The
preserve-or-reopen rule treats that failure as a first-class scientific
object. The Bell calibration then shows the complementary discipline:
even an exact and elegant new representation is not promoted into a new
physical mechanism when native mathematics explains the apparent novelty
completely.

The architecture therefore has coherent failure modes. It may survive as
an audit architecture while failing as foundational physics. A
state-space or recovery model may be useful without establishing an
independent information layer. A relational mechanism may close entirely
within P. These outcomes are scientifically preferable to protecting the
original ontology by semantic flexibility.

# 14. Conclusion

**possibility -> typed relation -> admissibility -> active physical law  
-> propagation and measurement -> readdressed possibility**

Across scale, the loop is extended by explicit comparison,
effective-object promotion, interface state, property-relative law
descent, separate closure and law-sufficiency margins, conditional
recovery, and preserve-or-reopen reuse. This is sufficient to state a
coherent, pressure-testable research architecture. It is not sufficient
to claim completed physical unification.

The decisive next work is concrete instantiation. This manuscript
supplies three entry points that can be attacked immediately - one exact
Bell calculation with a public computational verification package, one
prospective future-sufficiency discriminator, and one executable
matched-control traversal protocol - but none substitutes for the
foundational burden. A successful physical branch must still recover
established quantum and gravitational regimes and produce at least one
robust prospective discriminator beyond the strongest existing account.
Until then, MKUFT should be read as a relational architecture for
physical-law selection and cross-scale dynamics - not as accepted
physics, and not as a claim that an attractive formal pattern has
already become a law of nature.
