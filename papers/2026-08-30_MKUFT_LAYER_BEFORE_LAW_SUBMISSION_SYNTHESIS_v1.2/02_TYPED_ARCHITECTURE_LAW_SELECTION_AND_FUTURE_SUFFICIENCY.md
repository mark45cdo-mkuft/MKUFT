# 1. Scientific boundary and research status

The scientific status is deliberately narrow. The architecture is
designed to make strong claims harder to smuggle through notation,
analogy, or retrospective interpretation.

- MKUFT is an original layered research architecture using mostly
  established mathematical tools.

- Its current equations include notation, consistency conditions,
  research scaffolds, operational tests, and recovery obligations.

- No current foundational MKUFT equation is presented as a demonstrated
  new law of nature.

- No independent physical effect has yet established an S-layer or
  I-layer cause beyond an adequate physical-state, history, boundary,
  environment, and measurement model.

- A foundational MKUFT branch succeeds only if it recovers established
  physics and produces a discriminating result beyond strong
  alternatives.

# 2. Layer before law: the question prior to unification

Quantum theory and general relativity are highly successful in their
demonstrated regimes, yet their standard mathematical structures are not
straightforwardly unified. A conventional response is to search directly
for a deeper common dynamical law. MKUFT asks a prior question: before
selecting or combining laws, has the effective object, its state domain,
its construction address, its retained history, and the property whose
evolution is being predicted been typed correctly?

Every physical law already acts on a domain, under boundary and initial
conditions, with a specified representation and measurement route. If
those inputs change across scale, history, composition, or context, the
effective lawful object may change even when the underlying physics
remains entirely ordinary. A deeper claim - for example that
informational or relational structure has independent physical content -
must survive the strongest adequate physical description rather than
inherit causal status from useful description alone.

This is the meaning of Layer Before Law: object and relation
admissibility are tested before a residual is promoted into evidence for
law revision.

| **1** | What is the current effective possibility object?                                    |
|-------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **2** | Which relational and physical assignments are admissible?                            |
| **3** | What physical state domain, generator, and weighting are active?                     |
| **4** | How is the system propagated and measured?                                           |
| **5** | Which scale or layer owns the target property after update?                          |
| **6** | What observation forces reduction to an ordinary account or rejection of the branch? |

# 3. Typed state and the no-inheritance rule

**S -\> I -\> P -\> O**

These are typed roles, not four spatial dimensions and not four
automatically commensurable coordinates.

## S - substrate / structured possibility

An ambient possibility or constraint scaffold. A minimal mathematical
representation can be a measure space S = (Omega, Sigma, mu). This is a
representation of structured possibility, not evidence for a detected
material medium.

## I - information / relational constraint

Correlations, constraints, codes, relations, and organisational
structure. An information variable does not become an independent
physical cause merely because it is useful, compressive, or predictive.

## P - physical expression

Measurable states, fields, matter, energy, instruments, environments,
timing, and dynamics. Physical claims require physical variables, units,
conservation accounting where applicable, and comparison with the
strongest relevant physical baseline.

## O - observer-positioned registration

Measurement context, record, interpretation, memory, and bounded
observer-state variables when operationally defined. Ordinary
measurement back-action remains P-layer physics. Observer language
cannot replace a missing coupling.

## 3.1 Complete addressed state

```math
U_t=(S_t^{\mathrm{eff}},I_t,P_t,O_t,b_t,H_t)
```

Here b_t is the contextual or construction address and H_t is retained
load-bearing history. The tuple is bookkeeping until its components,
comparison maps, and couplings are defined. Its purpose is to prevent a
change in address, history, or measurement route from being mistaken for
motion inside one unchanged state space.

## 3.2 No untyped inheritance

```math
C_{LM}:\mathcal{X}_L\rightarrow\mathcal{X}_M
```

A lawful cross-address claim must identify the typed source and target
spaces, the relation claimed to survive the transfer, the translation or
coarse-graining used, the evidence appropriate to the receiving layer,
and an independent falsifier. Repeated algebraic form, visual
similarity, common graph shape, correlation, or shared vocabulary is not
a coupling.

# 4. The active law object and the SIPO capstone

The central construction separates admissibility from the physical law
that acts after admissibility has been established.

```math
\Xi_t=Q_t(U_t,E_t),
```

```math
\mathcal{L}_{P,t}=(D_{P,t},T_{P,t},W_{P,t}),
```

```math
C_P:(\Xi_t,P_t,E_t)\mapsto \mathcal{L}_{P,t}
```

Xi_t is an admissibility descriptor. The active physical-law object
L_P,t carries three things that are easily flattened in ordinary
notation: the lawful domain D_P,t, the transition rule or generator
T_P,t, and any domain-appropriate weighting or stochastic object W_P,t.
The decomposition prevents a familiar generator being applied outside
its domain and prevents a fitted weighting from silently assigning
probability to states that were never lawfully admissible.

|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 4.1 Compact operator form

```math
\mathrm{Update}_{\mathrm{SIPO}}=\mathrm{Readdress}\circ\mathrm{Instrument}_{P\to(P,O)}\circ\mathrm{Propagate}_{\mathcal{L}_P},
```

```math
\mathcal{L}_P=C_P\circ Q
```

This is the strongest compression of the architecture. It is
deliberately not presented as a finished physical law. Q and C_P must be
instantiated in a concrete regime. If they cannot be defined
prospectively without retrospective fitting, the foundational claim
fails. A physical measurement is typed P -\> (P,O) because the
instrument may alter the physical state while also creating an
observer-positioned record. Readdressing then recomputes the next
effective possibility object, contextual address, relation state,
promoted variables, and retained history.

# 5. Adaptive state spaces and lawful comparison

Learning, development, phase change, architectural change, and scale
transition can alter the variables, decoder, admissible operations, or
update rule. MKUFT therefore does not assume that every before/after
comparison lives inside one fixed state space. If the construction
address changes, subtraction, distance, or state identity requires an
explicit embedding, translation, quotient, or common observable;
otherwise the comparison is undefined rather than merely noisy.

- Within-address state equivalence is not the same object as
  address-class parity.

- Address-class parity is not the same object as cross-address identity
  continuity.

- A higher-scale readout remains a coarse-graining until the proposed
  higher object demonstrates persistence, interventionally load-bearing
  effects, viable capability, predictive compression, and
  boundary/closure specificity.

- Promotion changes the permitted model address; it does not erase
  lower-scale physics or prove autonomous higher-level causation.

# 6. Future-sufficient interfaces: preserve or reopen

A second failure mode appears after a local model has already passed its
original test. The model may later be reused as a component inside a
wider operation. A distinction that was irrelevant to the first task can
then become decision-bearing. Local correctness is therefore not
automatically future sufficiency.

## 6.1 Exact future-sufficiency criterion

```math
\Theta(h)=\Theta(h')\Longrightarrow
\mathcal{L}_{\lambda}(q^+_{\Delta}\mid h,u,E)=\mathcal{L}_{\lambda}(q^+_{\Delta}\mid h',u,E)
\quad \forall u\in\mathcal{U}
```

Theta maps retained histories into a candidate effective state. For a
target q, horizon Delta, admissible challenge family U, environment E,
and physical regime lambda, two histories may be compressed to the same
state only if every declared admissible challenge leaves their future
target distributions equivalent. If the wider operation can split those
supposedly identical states into materially different declared futures,
the state description was not sufficient for that use.

## 6.2 Preserve-or-reopen contract

```math
\mathrm{Admissible}_{F}(Y)\Longleftrightarrow
\mathrm{Preserve}_{F}(X,Y)\;\lor\;\mathrm{GuaranteedReopen}_{F}(Y\to X)
```

Let X be a lower relational object and Y = C_F(X) a domain-specific
compression used as an interface for a future-operation family F. The
interface can be deliberately incomplete and still lawful, but only if
every omitted relation is either irrelevant to the declared use or can
be restored before a downstream operation is allowed to depend on it.

1.  Preserve a recoverable provenance/descent address into the lower
    object.

2.  Declare the trigger for reopening before outcome inspection.

3.  Restore before consequential reuse, not after an error has already
    propagated.

4.  Recover the smallest dependency-complete lower cone required for the
    widened operation rather than replaying the entire history by
    default.

5.  Re-evaluate the upper closure after restoration.
