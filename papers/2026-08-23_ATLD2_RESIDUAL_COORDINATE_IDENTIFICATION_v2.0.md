## Abstract

Active Traversal and Load-Bearing Dependency (ATLD) v1.0 introduced a matched-control protocol for testing whether meaningful typed dependency architecture and active recursive traversal contribute causal functional gain beyond the same content presented through flat, isolated, semantically scrambled, or non-recursive controls. Its primary outcome vector contained seven readouts: task accuracy, multi-entry convergence, contradiction localisation, correction propagation, calibration, branch-failure localisation/repair, and efficiency plus evidence-path validity. The present paper asks a stricter successor question: can materially important long-horizon failures remain outside that seven-coordinate measurement body even when the original readouts are individually strong?

ATLD 2 preserves the causal grammar of v1.0 and treats measurement refinement as the object of study. A structural seven-on-seven self-application of the frozen v1.0 vector yields five candidate residual coordinates: exact object/address custody (O), permission/action-state integrity (U), future-sufficient continuity/re-entry fidelity (R), receiver-side closure (V), and parent fixed-point closure (F). The paper formalises each candidate as an observable over a matched task world, defines direct and mirror deformations, and gives selective-ablation, counterfactual-substitution, coalition, causal-shadow, and held-out diagnostic tests. The resulting candidate measurement body is then subjected to a twelve-on-twelve self-audit, but self-application is explicitly non-certifying: retention requires prospective selective deformation, unique diagnostic gain under a no-smuggling rule, neutral-domain transfer, and cross-model replication.

A derived route-support readout separates endpoint correctness from support for the declared traversal route using SUPPORTED, FAILED, and UNRESOLVED transition states and first-demonstrated-break localisation. A neutral synthetic “Release Relay” world demonstrates how the five candidate coordinates can be deformed one at a time without disclosing the private reasoning architecture that motivated their discovery. Causal-shadow accounting prevents one upstream fault from being counted as multiple independent failures, while bidirectional mirror tests prevent the new coordinates from becoming one-way optimisation targets. ATLD 2 therefore remains the same portable causal instrument as ATLD v1.0, but with a sharper measurement surface and a stricter burden for adding new dimensions. No empirical superiority or twelve-dimensional ontology is claimed. An exploratory 15-case Release Relay execution pilot is also reported using a Google Gemini Pro-mode trace and a non-blind private structured self-application. The pilot is a worked scoring application only: it does not validate the five candidate coordinates, does not establish system superiority, and does not change the confirmatory retention burden.

## What changed from ATLD v1.0

ATLD v1.0 remains the frozen causal predecessor. ATLD 2 does not replace its typed-corpus, matched-resource, active-traversal, relation-deformation, or matched-control grammar. It sharpens the measurement surface by proposing five candidate residual readouts:

- **O — exact object/address custody:** whether the system remains attached to the governing object and lawful lineage rather than drifting or locking to a stale object;
- **U — permission/action-state integrity:** whether action authority is neither overrun nor falsely prohibited;
- **R — future-sufficient continuity/re-entry fidelity:** whether interruption/recovery preserves the material lawful future rather than merely reproducing text;
- **V — receiver-side closure:** whether the intended receiver can reconstruct the correct object through the declared carrier rather than merely trusting source correctness;
- **F — parent fixed-point closure:** whether the system stops neither before parent-changing work is exhausted nor after no material gain remains.

The proposed twelve-coordinate body is not assumed to be a flat ontology. The original seven include performance/evidential readouts; O/U/R/V are integrity-state readouts; F is a two-sided terminal reachability/stopping readout. Candidates are retained only if they survive their declared empirical burden.

## Self-audit without self-certification

The paper makes a deliberate distinction between **structural recovery** and **empirical discovery**. The five candidates were already salient when the seven-on-seven reconstruction was performed, so that reconstruction is treated as convergent design evidence rather than blinded discovery. A stronger prospective test with independent evaluators is specified.

Coordinate retention additionally requires:

1. selective deformation under matched conditions;
2. direct and mirror failure detection;
3. coordinate removal and diagnostic-loss testing;
4. a no-smuggling rule preventing the evaluator from reintroducing the removed distinction under another label;
5. causal-shadow accounting so one upstream fault is not counted as multiple independent discoveries;
6. neutral-domain transfer and cross-model replication;
7. reduction, merger, or removal when the proposed coordinate adds no unique diagnostic value.

If all five candidates collapse under those tests, ATLD 2 reduces to a sharpened validation/security extension of ATLD v1.0 rather than insisting that twelve coordinates must survive.

## Exploratory execution pilot

A fixed 15-case Release Relay packet was used as a small exploratory execution pilot. The foreign comparator was Google Gemini: an initial Fast-mode run was followed by a second run with Pro mode explicitly selected; Deep Think was not enabled, and the Pro-mode trace is the scored foreign-system result. A non-blind private structured condition was also run on the same packet.

The pilot is intentionally bounded. It is not independent validation of the private condition, and it is not confirmatory validation of O/U/R/V/F. Its useful result is narrower: the scoring surface returned a shaped diagnostic profile rather than only an endpoint score. The foreign system remained strong across the five candidate residual coordinates while exposing a branch-localisation/route-support/calibration seam on RR-14. That residual was already owned by existing ATLD 2 machinery — L plus route support/first break, with movement in G — and therefore did **not** justify a thirteenth coordinate.

## Claim boundary

ATLD 2 is a methods/evaluation paper with an exploratory worked pilot. It does **not** claim:

- completed confirmatory validation of the five candidate coordinates;
- empirical superiority of the private structured condition;
- a universal twelve-dimensional ontology of AI reasoning;
- that internal self-audit is independent evidence;
- that a foreign-system pilot establishes general cross-model performance;
- or that the private reasoning implementation is disclosed by the public measurement protocol.
