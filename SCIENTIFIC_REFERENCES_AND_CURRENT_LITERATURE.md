## 1. Scope

This document collects load-bearing prior work, strong alternative programmes, experimental developments, and active scientific disputes relevant to MKUFT.

The presence of a citation does not imply confirmation of MKUFT. A cited work may serve as:

1. a direct scientific source;
2. an established result that MKUFT must recover or respect;
3. a neighbouring or parallel programme;
4. a strong alternative, criticism, or null model.

Technical modules retain local citations where a source supports a specific claim, equation, experiment, recovery requirement, or comparison. This document provides the broader literature map.

## 2. Current high-priority literature findings

### 2.1 Gravity-mediated entanglement remains a live and disputed discriminator

The 2017 Bose and Marletto–Vedral proposals established gravity-mediated entanglement as a serious laboratory route for probing nonclassical features of gravity.

The interpretation is not settled. Aziz and Howl (2025) argued that a local theory combining classical gravity with quantum-field-theoretic matter can generate entanglement with distinguishable scaling. Marletto, Oppenheim, Vedral, and Wilson subsequently disputed the claim that this constitutes entanglement mediated by classical gravity. Feng, Vedral, and Marletto also analysed collapse-based alternatives under the locality assumptions used by entanglement witnesses.

The resulting evidential position is narrower than “entanglement implies quantum gravity.” A positive result must be interpreted against the strongest classical–quantum, quantum-field-theoretic, collapse-based, and fully quantum alternatives, preferably through a discriminating scaling law or dynamical signature rather than the mere presence of entanglement.

This is directly relevant to [Layer Before Law](docs/26_LAYER_BEFORE_LAW_MKUFT_QUANTUM_GRAVITY_REFRAMING.md), whose physical claims must compete against the strongest available alternatives rather than a simplified classical caricature.

### 2.2 Architecture-first quantum-gravity work is scientifically legitimate but not automatically novel

Several established programmes place information capacity, global state-space structure, emergent geometry, operational axioms, or hybrid classical–quantum dynamics before the familiar effective description.

That literature supports the legitimacy of asking whether the conventional quantum-gravity problem has been categorised at the correct level. It does not establish the specific S–I–P–O architecture or make that architecture unique.

A distinct MKUFT contribution would therefore require at least one of the following: a sharper typed decomposition, a lawful coupling or recovery derivation, a discriminating prediction, a better experimental route, or a demonstrated cross-domain research advantage that survives matched alternatives.

### 2.3 Experimental gravity is moving toward mesoscopic and quantum-controlled regimes

Recent work includes lattice atom interferometry measuring gravitational attraction at laboratory scale, millimetre-scale quantum-phase proposals, optomechanical and levitated-mass routes toward gravity-mediated entanglement, single-graviton-sensitive resonator proposals, and witnesses designed to distinguish quantum gravitational behaviour from broader classical–quantum alternatives.

These developments improve the experimental neighbourhood in which MKUFT could state a specific mechanism and prediction. They do not remove the requirement to recover standard physics.

## 10. Evidence and citation standard

The evidential role of a reference should be identifiable from the surrounding argument. Primary scientific papers are preferred where available; preprints, reviews, commentaries, and news reports should remain distinguishable. A current technical dispute is represented as a dispute rather than resolved by citation count.

Novelty claims require comparison with the strongest close prior work at the level of mechanism or method, not merely terminology. A change in the strongest alternative, experimental feasibility, or interpretation of an existing proposed test changes the scientific context in which the relevant MKUFT branch should be evaluated.

## 13. Boundary statement

Neighbouring work makes the MKUFT research direction scientifically addressable; it does not validate the framework.

The current evidential position is:

> MKUFT is a distinct speculative research architecture positioned within legitimate scientific neighbourhoods. Its strongest claims remain unconfirmed until the proposed typed couplings are formalised, established limits are recovered, and at least one result discriminates the architecture from its strongest alternatives.
