## 1. Scope and evidential rule

This document locates MKUFT inside its scientific neighbourhood. A citation may supply an established result that MKUFT must recover, a serious alternative, a methodological tool, or a neighbouring programme. Citation does **not** mean confirmation of MKUFT.

Primary literature is preferred where possible. Live disputes are represented as disputes rather than resolved by citation count. Novelty is assessed against the strongest close prior work at the level of mechanism, mathematics, prediction, or experimental discrimination—not terminology alone.

## 10. Evidence and claim standard

For each cited neighbour, ask:

1. What result is established independently of MKUFT?
2. What constraint does that result place on MKUFT?
3. Is the relationship a source, prior work, strong alternative, structural analogue, or candidate experimental bridge?
4. What part of MKUFT remains unearned?
5. What observation would distinguish the MKUFT branch from the strongest alternative?

A changing literature can change the scientific context of an MKUFT branch. The public claim must change with it rather than preserving an outdated comparison.
