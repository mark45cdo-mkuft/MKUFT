## 1. Use rule

It does not replace local citations inside a technical module. A reader should not have to leave the module to identify the source supporting a load-bearing statement. Local references therefore remain appropriate where they support a specific claim, equation, experiment, recovery requirement, or strongest-fair-null comparison.


Citation does not imply confirmation of MKUFT. It records one of four relationships:

1. direct source;

## 2. Current high-priority literature findings

### 2.1 Gravity-mediated entanglement is a live and disputed discriminator

The 2017 Bose and Marletto–Vedral proposals established gravity-mediated entanglement as a serious laboratory route for probing nonclassical features of gravity.

The inference is no longer safe to state as universally settled. In 2025, Aziz and Howl argued that a local theory with classical gravity and quantum field theoretic matter can generate entanglement, with a scaling distinguishable from quantum-gravity predictions. Marletto, Oppenheim, Vedral, and Wilson subsequently disputed that conclusion, arguing that the entanglement in the proposed model is not mediated by classical gravity. Feng, Vedral, and Marletto also argued that collapse-based models evade the witness only by violating the locality assumptions on which it rests.

The sober MKUFT position is therefore:

> Gravity-mediated entanglement experiments remain highly relevant, but a positive entanglement result must be interpreted against the strongest classical–quantum, quantum-field-theoretic, nonlocal-collapse, and fully quantum alternatives. The experiment must target discriminating scaling or dynamics, not merely the presence of entanglement.

This directly strengthens the `Layer Before Law` requirement that MKUFT compete against the strongest fair null rather than an obsolete classical caricature.

### 2.2 Architecture-first quantum-gravity work is legitimate but not automatically novel

Several established programmes already place deeper architecture, information capacity, global state-space structure, emergent geometry, or hybrid classical–quantum dynamics before the familiar effective description.

This supports the legitimacy of asking whether the conventional quantum-gravity problem is categorised at the wrong level. It does not establish that the specific S–I–P–O architecture is correct or unique.

MKUFT must distinguish itself through at least one of:

- a sharper typed decomposition;
- a lawful coupling or recovery derivation;
- a discriminating prediction;
- a better experimental route;
- or a demonstrated cross-domain research advantage that survives matched alternatives.

### 2.3 Experimental gravity is moving toward mesoscopic and quantum-controlled regimes

Recent work includes:

- lattice atom interferometry measuring gravitational attraction at laboratory scale;
- proposals for millimetre-scale quantum-phase tests of gravity;
- optomechanical and levitated-mass routes toward gravity-mediated entanglement;
- gravitational-wave resonator proposals for single-graviton-sensitive signatures;
- and new witnesses intended to distinguish quantum gravitational behaviour from broader classical–quantum alternatives.

These developments improve the experimental neighbourhood in which MKUFT must state a specific mechanism and prediction. They do not remove the need for a complete recovery of standard physics.

## 10. Citation hygiene and update protocol

Before adding a paper to a technical argument:

1. identify whether it is a primary paper, review, commentary, preprint, or news report;
2. cite the primary paper wherever available;
3. distinguish peer-reviewed publication from preprint status;
4. state whether the work supports a result, provides a neighbour, supplies a null, or directly challenges the claim;
5. include both sides where a current technical dispute is load-bearing;
6. do not use citation volume as evidence that MKUFT is correct;
7. do not claim novelty until the strongest close prior work has been compared at mechanism level;
8. update this file when a new paper materially changes the strongest fair null, experimental feasibility, or interpretation of an existing proposed test.
